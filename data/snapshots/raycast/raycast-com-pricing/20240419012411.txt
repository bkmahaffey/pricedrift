FAQs
Answers to the most frequently asked questions.
How do you handle my data?
We value privacy and never collect any sensitive information. None of your inputs are recorded or used to train models. All Raycast AI features are powered by OpenAI's APIs. If Cloud Sync is enabled, all AI Chats are saved on our servers, so they can be accessed from any of your Macs.
What's the difference between the Pro and Team plan?
The Pro plan is for individuals. It includes Raycast AI, Cloud Sync and more. The Team plan is for organizations. On top of all Pro features, teams can share Extensions, Snippets and Quicklinks privately.
Can I use my own OpenAI API key?
We don't have an option to BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) for OpenAI. If you want to use your own key, you can use an Extension from the Store or build your own.
Which model do you use for Raycast AI?
Raycast Al provides access to multiple LLMs from state-of-the-art providers, including OpenAl, Anthropic, Meta, and more. More advanced models are available as an add-on. Find out more on the Pricing page.
Can I upgrade my Teams subscription to use Advanced AI?
Yes! A Team Manager can add the AI+ add-on by going to their account settings. Just keep in mind that this will activate the add-on for all the members of your team.
Does Raycast have a Student Program?
Yes, Raycast offers a Pro plan 50% discount to verified students. You can apply for it by completing this form
Please note that the student discount only applies to the Raycast Pro plan, and not the Advanced AI plan.
