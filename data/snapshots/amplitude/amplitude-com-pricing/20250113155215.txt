Frequently asked questions
If you have a question about our plans or pricing structure, get in touch with a product specialist.
1. What is an MTU?
An MTU (monthly tracked user) is a unique user with one or more events in an organization within a calendar month. It can be either an anonymous user (which Amplitude will track via device ID) or an identified user (tracked by user ID).
2. How can I estimate MTUs?
MTU count is similar to the Monthly Active Users (MAU) count on your website or applications, provided they account for anonymous visitors identically. For more information on how to estimate and track MTUs, please refer to our MTU Help Center article
.
3. How do I get access to Session Replay, CDP and Experimentation?
Amplitude offers four plans that put the full power of our platform into your teams’ hands. Our Starter and Plus plans are meant for small teams and come packed with Analytics, feature flags, Session Replay, and CDP. Our Growth and Enterprise plans unlock the full power of our platform, including experimentation and advanced audience management, to help you scale with deeper insights, automation, and scale.
4. Do you offer special pricing or discounts for startups?
Yes! The Amplitude Startup Scholarship provides one year of our paid Growth plan features for free.
