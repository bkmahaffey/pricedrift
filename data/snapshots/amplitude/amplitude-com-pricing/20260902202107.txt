Frequently asked questions
Does Amplitude have a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan is completely free, with no credit card required. You get 2 million events per month, plus access to out-of-the-box analytics, session replay, unlimited feature flags, web experimentation, activation, guides and surveys, unlimited AI Agents, MCP, AI Visibility, and AI Feedback. It's not a trial. There's no time limit. You can use Amplitude for free as long as it fits your needs.
How much does Amplitude cost?
Amplitude has plans for every stage of growth. The Amplitude Free plan includes 2 million events per month, session replay, A/B tests, feature flags, AI analytics, and unlimited seats, with no credit card required and no time limit. The Amplitude Plus plan starts at $0 and scales with your event volume as you grow. You can track usage in real time and upgrade anytime for better rates. For larger teams, the Growth and Enterprise plans offer custom pricing based on your volume and feature needs
What is a monthly tracked user (MTU)?
An MTU is a unique user who triggers one or more events in your product within a calendar month. One MTU can generate many events, which is why Amplitude's Free plan measures usage by event volume, with 2 million events included per month.
What is an event?
An event in Amplitude is any action a user takes in your product: a button click, a page view, a signup, a purchase. Every time a user does something you want to track, that's one event. The Free plan includes up to 2 million events per month, which is more than enough for most teams getting started.
Does Amplitude's free plan require a credit card?
You don't need a credit card to get started in Amplitude. The Free plan requires no credit card at all. You only need to add one when you upgrade to Plus and start growing beyond the free limits.
What's the difference between Free and Plus?
Free helps you get started, and Plus helps you scale. Free comes with a fixed limit of 2 million events per month, perfect for learning and finding product-market fit. Plus unlocks higher event volume, better pricing as you grow, and the full platform for deeper insights, experimentation, and activation. You only pay when your product starts delivering value.
Can I use Amplitude data inside other AI tools?
Yes, Amplitude connects to Claude, Cursor, Lovable, Figma Make, and any tool that supports MCP (Model Context Protocol). You can ask questions about your product data, build features informed by real usage, or generate reports without leaving the tools you already use. This works on every plan, including the Free plan.
Does Amplitude offer discounts for startups?
Yes. The Startup Scholarship provides one free year of the Growth plan for companies with fewer than 20 employees and less than $10M in funding. The Tech for Black Founders Scholarship offers free, unlimited access to the Growth plan for qualifying organizations. Includes advanced analytics, experimentation, governance, and dedicated onboarding. No credit card to apply.
What's included in Amplitude beyond analytics?
Every Amplitude plan, including the Free plan, gives you access to the full platform: analytics, session replay, feature experimentation, web experimentation, guides and surveys, activation, AI Feedback, AI Visibility, AI Agents, and AI Assistant. On Free, these come with limited volumes and capabilities. Is Amplitude worth it for small teams or early-stage startups?
Absolutely. The Free plan gives you 2 million events per month and access to the entire platform, including analytics, session replay, feature flags, web experimentation, guides and surveys, activation, AI Feedback, AI Agents, and AI Visibility, all at no cost with limited volumes. If you qualify for the Startup Scholarship, you can get a full year of the Growth plan for free. Many startups run Amplitude at zero cost for their first one to two years, and only upgrade when they're ready.
What's the difference between the Growth and Enterprise plans?
The Growth plan is built for teams that need advanced analytics, causal insights, SSO and project permissions, and real-time data streaming. The Enterprise plan adds dedicated support, a customer success manager, advanced security, advanced user management, RBAC (role based access control), and compliance features, and custom data retention. Both plans are custom-priced based on your volume and requirements.
Does Amplitude offer a free trial?
Amplitude offers something better than a trial: a free plan with no time limit. The Free plan gives you 2 million events per month, with access to core analytics, session replay, feature flags, and more. There's no credit card required to get started. Use it for as long as you need.
