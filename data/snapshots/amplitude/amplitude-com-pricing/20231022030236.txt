Frequently Asked Questions
If you have a question about our plans or pricing structure, get in touch with a product specialist.
1. What is an MTU?
An MTU (monthly tracked user) is a unique user who triggers one or more events in a project in a calendar month. It can be either an anonymous user (which Amplitude will track via device ID) or an identified user (tracked by user ID).
2. How can I estimate MTUs?
MTU count is similar to the Monthly Active Users (MAU) count on your website or applications, provided they account for anonymous visitors identically. For more information on how to estimate and track MTUs, please refer to our MTU help center article here
.
3. How can I get access to CDP & Experimentation?
Starter and Plus plans are platform offerings, and come with Analytics, Experiment, and CDP. You will get access to feature management, identity resolution, audience targeting, and more. Refer to the detailed comparison table to see what’s included in each plan.
4. Do you offer special pricing or discounts for startups?
Yes! The Amplitude Startup Scholarship provides one year of our paid Growth plan features for free. Learn more and apply here
.
