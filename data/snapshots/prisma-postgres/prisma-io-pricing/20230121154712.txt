Frequently asked questions
Can I upgrade or downgrade the project plan at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade your project to the Scale plan and downgrade at any time, by contacting our support team using the in-app messaging widget located at the bottom right of the Prisma Data Platform UI.
Is it possible to pay annually?
Yes, it is possible to pay annually on our Enterprise plans. Get in touch with our Support team via the in-app messaging widget directly in the Prisma Data Platform.
Is it possible to pay by invoice instead of credit card?
Yes, we support invoice payments on our Enterprise plans. Get in touch with our Support team via the in-app messaging widget directly in the Prisma Data Platform.
How does billing work?
Billing occurs on a monthly basis aligned with calendar months. You are billed and receive invoices for each month on the 1st. Each invoice bills upfront the usage until the end of that month.
The first invoice is at a pro-rated amount of the monthly plan price. This means that we bill you only for the period during which your project uses the paid plan during the first month.
Read more about how billing works.
How are team members and environments computed and billed?
For projects on the Free plan, the number of team members and environments is limited.
For projects on the Scale plan, when using team members and environments beyond the quota included, overages are pro-rated by the day.
Read more about how billing works.
How is data proxy usage metered and billed?
Data Proxy usage is metered by CPU milliseconds across all environments in the project. This corresponds to the aggregate of actual CPU time that the Prisma Data Platform spent processing, not the time spent waiting.
Projects on the Free plan have hard limits in terms of Data Proxy usage, meaning that no overages will be charged, but the Data Proxy will be paused past that point until the next billing cycle. You will receive multiple warnings before reaching these limits.
For project on the Scale plan, any additional CPU milliseconds going above the plan quota will be charged as overages and included in the monthly bill at the end of the billing cycle.
Read more about how billing works.
