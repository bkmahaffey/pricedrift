Frequently asked questions FAQs
Is it possible to pay annually?
You can opt for annual payment with our Enterprise plans. Please contact our Support team at support@prisma.io for further details.
Is it possible to pay by invoice instead of credit card?
Yes, we support invoice payments with our Enterprise plans. Please contact our Support team at support@prisma.io for further details.
Do we have to pay for using the Prisma ORM?
No, there are no payments required. Our open-source ORM is and will remain free.
If you have any questions, please reach out to our support team at support@prisma.io
