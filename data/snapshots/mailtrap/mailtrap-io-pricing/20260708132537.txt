Frequently Asked Questions
What’s the difference between Email API/SMTP and Email Sandbox products?
The main difference is that Email API/SMTP is built for developers and DevOps teams to deliver production emails directly to customer inboxes. Email Sandbox provides dev and QA teams a safe environment to inspect and debug emails in development or staging, capturing outgoing messages so they never reach real customers.
What’s the difference between Email API/SMTP and Email Marketing products?
Email API/SMTP allows you to send both transactional and bulk emails via SMTP or API integrations. Email Marketing is designed to build, schedule, and manage campaigns directly through the Mailtrap interface and contact list manager.
What happens if I hit the limits of my Email API/SMTP plan?
If you reach your Email API/SMTP monthly sending limit, you’ll receive the SMTP protocol error: “535 5.7.0 Monthly messages limit reached”. To continue sending emails, you’ll need either to upgrade your subscription plan, or wait until the next billing period when the limit resets.
Do you provide help with setting up Mailtrap for sending?
Yes, Mailtrap provides setup instructions for a range of domain providers and a getting started guide available in our help center to assist you with the Email API/SMTP or the Email Marketing setup process. Mailtrap’s deliverability team
is also available for setup assistance, and the support team can answer all related questions.
Do you help with migration from another sending provider?
Yes, Mailtrap’s deliverability and tech experts provide dedicated migration support for senders with volumes exceeding 200K, with a strategy tailored to their business needs. For smaller-scale senders, we offer free migration guides to facilitate a smooth transition.
Do I need to buy Email API/SMTP, Email Marketing and Email Sandbox separately?
Yes, Email API/SMTP, Email Marketing and Email Sandbox are sold separately, all offering a free plan and multiple paid options.
