Frequently Asked Questions
What happens if I reach the limits of my plan?
If you reach your monthly limit, you’ll receive the SMTP protocol error: “535 5.7.0 Monthly messages limit reached”. To continue sending emails, you’ll need either to upgrade your subscription plan, or wait until the next billing period when the limit resets.
Do I need to buy two plans for Email API/SMTP and Email Marketing separately?
No, a single subscription plan includes both Email API/SMTP and Email Marketing. The email-sending quota within your subscription plan covers transactional, marketing, and bulk emails, meaning you pay for the number of emails you send regardless of the email type.
Do you provide help with setting up Mailtrap for sending?
Yes, Mailtrap provides setup instructions for a range of domain providers and a getting started guide available in our help center to assist you with the Email API/SMTP setup process. Mailtrap’s deliverability team
is also available for setup assistance, and the support team can answer all related questions.
Do you help with migration from another sending provider?
Yes, Mailtrap’s deliverability and tech experts provide dedicated migration support for senders with volumes exceeding 200K, with a strategy tailored to their business needs. For smaller-scale senders, we offer free migration guides to facilitate a smooth transition.
What’s the difference between Email Sandbox and Email API/SMTP products?
The main difference between these tools is that Email Sandbox is used for inspecting and debugging emails with a fake SMTP, and does not send emails to real addresses, while Email API/SMTP and Email Marketing services deliver emails directly to customer inboxes.
Do I need to buy Email Sandbox and Email API/SMTP separately?
Yes, Email Sandbox and Email API/SMTP are sold separately, both offering a free plan and multiple paid options.
