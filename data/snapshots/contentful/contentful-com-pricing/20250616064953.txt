FAQs
Here to help with any questions you may have about our plans and pricing.
What products are available in Contentful?
Contentful Platform is the foundation of a powerful content ecosystem, enabling teams to create, manage, and scale structured content across all digital channels. It integrates seamlessly with other Contentful products like Personalization, Studio, and AI Actions.
Contentful Personalization is an AI-native personalization engine that empowers teams to experiment, target, and analyze content experiences — directly within Contentful, based on any data source.
Contentful Studio is visual workspace for designing digital experiences. It offers reusable templates, content binding, and design controls to scale creative work while maintaining brand consistency.
Contentful AI Actions automates content creation, translation, and transformation — speeding up workflows with contextual, adaptable, AI-powered tools.
Do I need a development team to setup Contentful products?
Most Contentful products offer no-code tools so that marketers can create and manage experiences directly in the platform without developer assistance. AI Actions, for instance, is ready to use out of the box. No setup needed. For products like Studio and Personalization, however, some initial development is typically required to integrate with your front end and tailor the experience to your stack.
Do you need help getting up and running? Our Professional Services
team would be happy to assist you.
How much do Contentful products cost?
We offer a customizable range of plans to suit teams, projects, and business of all sizes. To find the right plan for you, please contact our sales team
.
How do the products work together?
Contentful products are built to work seamlessly across the entire content management lifecycle:
Platform is the core for content management and delivery. It enables teams to create, manage, and scale content effortlessly across multiple digital channels. Acting as the central hub, it connects all other products, ensuring consistent content flow and accessibility.
Studio enables teams to design and build digital experiences visually. It extends functionality to meet creative and technical needs with design templates, reusable components, and robust developer tools.
Personalization ensures content resonates with specific audiences. Using audience segmentation and dynamic content delivery, teams can craft relevant experiences, all powered within the same platform.
AI Actions automates content creation, transformation, and localization. Whether it’s generating new content, translating for different regions, optimizing for search, or refining workflows, AI Actions brings speed and efficiency to the entire ecosystem, empowering teams to balance creativity with productivity.
Together, they create a unified, collaborative environment where teams can design, personalize, and scale digital experiences efficiently.

How do Spaces work?
A space is a content repository. Think of it as a place to keep all the content related to one team, product line, geography or project. Each space has its own content model, content base and user permissions.
If you want to launch several websites or apps, the best way to go is to create a separate space for each project.
Note that if your goal is to deliver the same content to multiple platforms – a typical set would be web, iOS app and Android app – then you should use one space and create multiple API keys to deliver the content.
