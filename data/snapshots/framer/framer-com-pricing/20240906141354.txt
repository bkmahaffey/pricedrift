FAQ
How are additional editors billed?
Additional editors are billed at a workspace level depending on the workspace plan and billing term selected (yearly or monthly). Any editors added after the initial purchase will be charged monthly. Please note that the workspace owner is free. Editors can be added in any time from the invite panel in your project or via your team settings. You can read more about roles here
.
Can I trial Framer before paying?
You can test Framer on the free plan where you can experiment with unlimited projects.
What payment methods do you offer?
We support payment through credit card and Paypal. Invoicing and purchase orders are only available with our Enterprise plan.
Can I cancel my site plan at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through the app. Subscriptions will be canceled as of the next billing cycle. If you cancel, your plan will be downgraded to the free plan. You won’t be locked out of any projects, but you will lose access to paid features. Enterprise plans can be cancelled as agreed in the order form.

What are Site and Workspace plans?
There are two main types of plans you can purchase through our website: Site and Workspace Plans.
Site Plans - These are essential for each project you wish to develop on Framer. The plans include features, hosting services, and all the paid options enable you to link a custom domain for your project.
Workspace Plans - Your Workspace area in Framer is where you can view and manage all your projects. Workspace plans are designed to add more members to your team, facilitating collaboration on projects. Moreover, they give you extra features, such as Custom Code across all projects.
You can read all about them here
.
