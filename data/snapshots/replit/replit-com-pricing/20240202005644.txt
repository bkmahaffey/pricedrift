Deployments pricing
Pricing differs between our three Deployments offerings: Autoscale, Reserved VM, and Static
Autoscale
Starting at $0.000024 / second
Autoscale is billed based on your resource usage. Your app's servers automatically scale from zero to any level of demand, and the work the servers do is measured in compute units.
A compute unit measures both the amount of time and the machine power used (CPU + RAM) to complete a task. You pick your app’s machine power, so if you choose a more powerful machine, it will use more compute units per second while it is running. Each compute unit costs $ 0.00000125. More on compute units
6 million compute units per month are included in Replit Core. You will be notified if you come close to using all your compute units in a month.
In addition to compute units, Autoscale deployments are billed for requests. 2.5 Million requests per month are included in Replit Core. If you use all the compute units or requests from your plan, any additional usage will be billed at the end of the month or once you accumulate $10 of usage. Compute unit overages are charged at $ 0.00000125 per compute unit. Any request overages are charged at $ 0.4 per million requests. You can always check your current usage for the month in your Account
page.
