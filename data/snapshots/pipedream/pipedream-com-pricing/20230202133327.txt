FAQ What are invocations?
Pipedream counts an invocation each time a workflow or event source is triggered by an incoming event.
Pipedream increments the count of invocations by one for each incoming event, regardless of the number of steps in your workflow. For example, if you send an HTTP request to a workflow with five steps, Pipedream will count that as one invocation.

On the Developer tier, you will also receive an email when you are at 80% and 100% of your invocations and compute time limits

Can I pay for Pipedream on an annual basis?
Yes, Teams and Enterprise customers can pay for Pipedream annually. Please reach out to billing@pipedream.com for more information.
Can I add other people to my account?
Yes, you can add other people to your account by creating an organization
and adding team members to your organization in your Settings.
Are there any limits on the paid plan?
Paid plans are not limited on invocations or compute time. However, other platform limits
(memory, QPS, etc.) apply. Some of these limits can be raised. Please reach out to our team
to discuss.
What if I want to change my plan?
You can change your plan in the Billing
page within your Settings
.
How do I cancel my paid plan?
You can cancel your plan in the Billing
page within your Settings

Do you have a discount for open source projects?
If you are working on an open source project and need free or discounted resources from Pipedream, feel free to contact us
.
Can I pay by invoice, instead of credit / debit card?
Yes, Pipedream can issue invoices on the Enterprise Plan. Invoices are paid annually.
