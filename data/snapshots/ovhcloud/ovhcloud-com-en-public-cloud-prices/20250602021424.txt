The minimum archive duration is 180 days. If an archive is deleted before this commitment period (with ‘date’ being between 0 and 180 days), the customer will be charged an additional fee, calculated as follows: (180 days — date) x storage class price.
Even if you have less than 1 TB of data, archiving a container will still be charged as if you had 1 TB of stored data. Simply put, the minimum amount billed for archiving is the 1 TB rate. The archiving operation is free.

If you archive a 500 GB bucket, you’ll be charged the 1 TB rate.
If you archive a 1500 GB bucket, you’ll be charged the 1 TB + 500 GB rate

The OVHcloud Data Platform offers pricing based on four dimensions: the computing power dedicated to query execution, data processing, application hosting, and the volume of data stored.
The computing power unit used to perform data processing and host applications is the Data Platform Unit (DPU), corresponding to 1 vCPU and 4 GB RAM.
With the OVHcloud Data Platform, you are billed per organisation, not per user. This way, you can add as many employees and end users to your organisation as you want.

Pricing model
Pay-as-you-go
All of our Public Cloud solutions are billed based on actual usage, which means you only pay for what you use. You launch the resources you need, when you need them, with no hidden costs. At the end of the month, we calculate your final bill, based on the resources you have used over that period, and deliver it as a single, clear invoice.
Contract duration
On-demand resources are non-binding. You can remove a resource at any time, after which you will not be billed for it.
Likewise, instances with monthly billing can be stopped at any time, and only the months you use are billed (calculated on the basis of an instance existing on the 1st day of the month, at 00:00).
Segmentation by project
For simplified administration of your operations, you can compartmentalise your Public Cloud solutions by project.
One invoice
You will receive only one invoice per project. This is sent at the beginning of each month, based on the previous month’s usage.
