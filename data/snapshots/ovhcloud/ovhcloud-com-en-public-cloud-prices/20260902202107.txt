The OVHcloud Data Platform offers pricing based on four dimensions: the computing power dedicated to query execution, data processing, application hosting, and the volume of data stored.
The computing power unit used to perform data processing and host applications is the Data Platform Unit (DPU), corresponding to 1 vCPU and 4 GB RAM.
With the OVHcloud Data Platform, you are billed per organisation, not per user. This way, you can add as many employees and end users to your organisation as you want.

With this on-demand model, billing is based on:
the number of TB of data processed by each request, whether they are carried out in the data lakehouse or in Direct Query (to data outside the Data Platform) from the Analytics Manager
the number of DPUs used to perform actions with the Data Processing Engine or Application Services Usage is measured per second and billed per hour.
For each of your actions or applications, you can increase the number of DPUs allocated to speed up execution and improve processing reliability.
Storage pricing is the cost of retaining the data you upload to the Data Platform Data Lakehouse, measured in gigabytes (GB) of stored data. OVHcloud does not charge any additional fees for data transfer (incoming or outgoing).

Pricing model
Pay-as-you-go
All of our Public Cloud solutions are billed based on actual usage, which means you only pay for what you use. You launch the resources you need, when you need them, with no hidden costs. At the end of the month, we calculate your final bill, based on the resources you have used over that period, and deliver it as a single, clear invoice.
Contract duration
On-demand resources are non-binding. You can remove a resource at any time, after which you will not be billed for it. Likewise, instances with monthly billing can be stopped at any time, and only the months you use are billed (calculated on the basis of an instance existing on the 1st day of the month, at 00:00).
Segmentation by project
For simplified administration of your operations, you can compartmentalise your Public Cloud solutions by project.
