FAQs
What’s the difference between pod-based or serverless indexes?
Pod-based indexes let you decide up front how much storage capacity and compute resources to use. Choose pod-based indexes if you’re certain about your workload and require throughput of more than 5 Queries Per Second (QPS) on a single namespace. Recommender systems typically fit this description. Pod-based indexes are billed hourly based on the number and type of pods used.
Serverless indexes let you start and scale with ultimate ease and flexibility, without provisioning resources. They use our next-generation architecture, algorithms, and API to provide the best developer experience, scalability, and cost efficiency, especially for larger indexes with lower throughputs per namespace. Multi-tenant RAG and labeling/classification workloads typically fit this description. Serverless indexes are billed for the usage of reads, writes, and storage.

When will serverless be generally available on AWS, on GCP, and Azure?
Pinecone serverless will soon be generally available in AWS regions, followed by public-preview availability for Azure and GCP regions.
Pod-based indexes are generally available across multiple regions in AWS, Azure, and GCP.
What regions are currently supported, and will additional regions be added in the future?
Pinecone serverless is currently available on AWS regions ‘us-east-1’, ‘us-west-2’, and ‘eu-west-1’. Support for additional regions is coming soon.
