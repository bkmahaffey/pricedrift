What is a Search request?
A Search request is a group of one or more search operations sent in a single exchange. Search operations are triggered when a search is performed. In autocomplete and search-as-you-type implementations, a new search request is performed on every keystroke.
In past Algolia plans, a single search would be counted per active search index. So Algolia would count a single search applied to 3 active indexes as 3 search “operations”. With the current pricing, in this example Algolia would count this as one search “request”. This greatly simplifies our pricing model for customers using federated search and for customers with multiple sorts (e.g. newest products, lowest cost, highest cost, etc.).
For more details, please refer to our detailed documentation
.
What is a record? How do you count records?
Simply put, a record is an item or object that contains data that someone would search (or get recommendations) for. The total number of records is equal to the sum of records that you have in all of your indices. Note that each pre-sort of your records creates a copy of the relevant pre-sorted records and as a result, increases your total record count.
Each Search plan allows you to store a certain number of records at any given time. You can update, delete, or add as many records as you want as long as the total number of the indexed records does not exceed the inclusion of the plan. If you go over the inclusion, you’ll be charged an over-quota based on the maximum number of records you’ve reached. Depending on your plan, there may be an opportunity to pre-purchase additional records at the beginning of your contract period at a discounted pr
Note: To offer flexibility, Algolia ignores the 3 days with the most records in each month before calculating the total number of records.
What is a Recommend request?
A Recommend request is triggered when a Recommend API call is performed to retrieve a list of recommendations for a specific item. Multiple Recommend requests can be batched within a single Recommend API call to improve network latency.
What is a Search unit?
A Search unit is used to represent a certain number of search requests (query only, free indexing ops) and records.
A Search unit allows us to provide one metric per price level, instead of two separate ones, in an effort to simplify pricing for our customers.
How many Search requests and records are included per Search unit?
When using Pay-as-you go pricing (no contract or minimum commitment), each unit contains 1,000 search requests per month, and a maximum of 1,000 records. This way, one Search unit bought each month for twelve months will give 12,000 searches in one year, with a maximum of 1,000 records at any point in the year.
When committing annually, you will get 12 Search units over a 12-month period including 12,000 search requests for twelve months and a maximum of 1,000 records at any point in the year.
The minimum commitment for a year is 1,200 Search units.
If your use case includes higher record volumes than what is provided by this model, please contact sales
.
If fewer Search units are used than included in what I purchased, they do not roll over to the following service period.
How are requests powering Browse and Navigation experiences counted?
Browse and Navigation experiences rely on the same Search requests as search results pages, but are instead powered by a click (of a page or filter) rather than a query string.
Who owns my data?
You do. You retain ownership of all the data you send to your user account.
What happens if I exceed my committed usage?
If you exceed your committed usage overages will be charged. For Standard Search plans, the cost is $1 / extra unit. For Premium Search plans, the cost is $1.50 / extra unit. For Recommend, the cost is $0.60 / extra 1,000 Recommend requests.

Can I extend the retention period of the analytics data?
Yes, we are providing 90-day retention for data for all new customers and can extend to 365-day as an add-on.
How does pricing scale as I use Algolia for more Search requests?
Algolia’s pricing offers volume-based discounts and committed use discounts.
There are three main levers to consider - how many Search units will be consumed, the product edition in use (Standard or Premium) and whether or not you are using Algolia in a pay-as-you-go (PAYGO) model, or annual commitment model.
When committing to the equivalent of 100 Search units per month or more, you automatically unlock a discount compared to PAYGO. Please contact sales
for more information.
What are the advantages of an annual commitment vs. pay-as-you-go?
Annual commitments allow you to unlock discounts on your Algolia usage. For a minimum of 100 Search units per month / 1,200 Search units per year (or 100,000 Recommend requests per month / 1,200,000 Recommend requests per year), you will receive a discount. For additional Search units above and beyond 500 Search units per month, you may unlock even greater volume-based discounts, making your cost per Search unit even less.
