Frequently Asked Questions
What is an event?
Statsig records an event when your application calls the Statsig SDK to check whether a user should be exposed to a feature gate or experiment, to log a custom event (to power a metric), or to check a dynamic config.
Statsig dedupes billable exposure events for the same user and feature/experiment on client (60m window; proxy for session duration) and server SDKs (1m window; proxy for user request handling).
Experiment checks that result in no allocation (e.g. the experiment hasn’t started, or has finished) or Feature Gates that have been disabled (fully shipped or abandoned with no rule evaluation) do not generate billable exposure events.
How do I get started with the Statsig?
When you create a Statsig account, you automatically start with Statsig’s Always Free, On-demand tier where you can log up to 5 million events every calendar month for no charge.
Can I roll over any free, unused events into the next month?
No, if you don’t log up to 5 million events in a given calendar month, the remaining events balance doesn’t roll over to the next month. To maximize your On-demand benefits, we encourage you to log as many events as required to adequately capture the impact of the product improvements you’re testing.
What happens if I exceed the free events limit?
If you exceed the free events limit, you’ll start to accrue a charge at the On-demand rate with built-in volume discounts. For a given month, you accrue a charge only for events that exceed the free limit that month. Statsig alerts you via email when your usage exceeds the free limit using the email address that you used to create your Statsig account. When will Statsig charge me?
Statsig generates an invoice for the previous month at the beginning of each new month. When you wish to submit a payment for a given invoice, we accept your payment method via Stripe and charge the amount listed in the invoice that you wish to pay.
How do I monitor my On-demand usage?
To monitor your On-demand usage, navigate to the Project Settings and click on the Usage & Billing tab on the Statsig console at https://console.statsig.com
