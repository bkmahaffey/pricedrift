Frequently asked questions
How can I upgrade my account?
You can upgrade your account at any time to get more volume or more features (or both) from within your account settings. You can also opt-in to auto-upgrades, and we’ll automatically move you up to another plan if the upgrade would actually be cheaper given your current monthly usage of emails and validations.
How does Mailgun work?
Mailgun is an email delivery and deliverability service that empowers businesses to easily send email from their applications. We’ve built a powerful, cloud-based infrastructure that scales gracefully and handles billions of emails per month so you don’t have to deal with the complexity and hassle of maintaining your own servers. It’s free to sign up and easy to get started. Read our docs for more info on how Mailgun works.
How does your billing work?
Upon signup, you will only be billed the prorated amount of the selected plan for the current month. After that, your monthly plan cost and any extra emails and validations you use will be billed at the start of each month for the previous month. If you decide to cancel your account, any remaining balance due will be charged before the account can be closed.
How do I send my emails with Mailgun?
Since Mailgun exposes both an HTTP API and an SMTP endpoint, you can choose to send the way you’d like. Both ways have their advantages… SMTP is easy to setup and often plug and play, but API is much more reliable at scale. If you’re building an application, we recommend using our HTTP API.
One thing to note: There’s no send button in Mailgun, so if that’s what you’re looking for, contact us and we’ll point you in the right direction.
How many dedicated IPs do I need? Can I get more?
While some customers are happy on our shared IPs, others prefer the benefit of dedicated IPs, which give you more control over your reputation and deliverability. One dedicated IP is included with Foundation 100k, Growth and Scale plans.
The number of dedicated IPs you need depends on your volume, the types of email you're sending, and your frequency. Considering no two senders are alike, it can be hard to give a blanket recommendation, but with Mailgun Optimize's Deliverability Services
your TAM can investigate and advise on the exact number and types of IPs that best suite your business. Otherwise, you can add additional IPs to your account for $59/IP/month from within the control panel or by contacting support.
How many users come with each plan?
Every Mailgun plan (except free) allows you to add as many users as you need to your account at no additional cost. We find that this keeps people from sharing login credentials (a security no-no), and it makes you happy. Win win. You can also manage user permissions with our five user roles. Read more about our role-based access control
Free plans are limited to 1 user per account.
I want to test out Mailgun first. How can I do that?
The Trial plan is ideal for those looking to test out the service. On the foundation trial, you get a 30-day free trial of everything included in the Foundation plan. After 30-days, you will be upgraded to the Foundation 50k plan. Trial provides you with the following features:
5 days of log retention so you can easily troubleshoot issues
24/7 ticket support to get help any time of day, plus access to comprehensive documentation
Access to rich data to troubleshoot and optimize your transactional and marketing email performance
