FAQs
How do I get access to SMS?
SMS marketing is available to Mailchimp users in the United States and United Kingdom with a paid marketing plan. After you agree to the terms, submit an SMS Marketing application
and get approved, you can purchase SMS credits from the SMS overview page
the SMS settings page
the SMS editor, or through an email link sent to the primary email address
associated with your Mailchimp account.
Learn more about SMS marketing
How do SMS credits work?
Credits are purchased in blocks as an add-on to a paid monthly marketing plan. Credit blocks are automatically re-purchased each month on the account billing date. Unused credits will expire each month and do not roll over. You can add credits as your subscribed SMS audience grows or your sending frequency increases. You can also make a one-time purchase in the middle of a billing cycle if you run out of credits early and want to refill them before your credits renew.
Learn more about SMS marketing credits
Can I buy email credits instead of a monthly marketing plan?
Yes. If you send emails infrequently and prefer to pay as you go, you can buy email credits as an alternative to a monthly plan.
See how it works
How do I switch to a different plan?
You can switch to a new plan at any time by going to the Billing page in your account. Select the plan you’d like to switch to and immediately start using new features.
What payment methods do you offer?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and (in select countries) direct debit. We also support transactions in a range of currencies.
What happens if I hit the contact or sending limit?
After your Free Trial, if you hit your contact or sending limit, Mailchimp will automatically bill you for your overages. As long as you pay for your overages on time, we will not stop your campaigns or pause your account. In addition, if your audience size grows to (or email sends reach) the point where it would be more cost effective for you to be in a different contact tier during your Free Trial, Mailchimp may upgrade your contact tier so you are in a more economical tier when billing begins
Do you have pricing for high-volume email senders?
We do indeed. If you have more than 200,000 contacts, we have a high-volume plan that can meet your needs.
Contact us for pricing
