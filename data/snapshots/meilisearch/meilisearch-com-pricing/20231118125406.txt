FAQ
How do I choose the right plan?
Choosing the right plan depends on your use case, and the number of documents and search requests your app will consume. The Build plan is suitable for small businesses or individuals whose needs and workload are not yet at the level of the Pro plan. It’s a great option if you’re building out your solution and don’t yet have the level of usage required to justify the cost of a Grow or Pro plan. The costs associated with the Build plan scale with your usage, so it’s a good fit for growing busines
What happens if I exceed my plan's included usage?
If you exceed your plan's included usage, you will be charged for additional usage based on the number of searches performed and documents stored. Additional documents cost $0.05 for every 1,000 documents over the number of documents included in your plan. Additional search requests cost $0.20 for every 1,000 searches over the number of searches included in your plan.
Is there a minimum term or commitment once I start paying?
There is no minimum term, and users can close their accounts anytime. Users will only be charged for the period during which a project was active. How do we accept payment?
We only accept payment online via credit cards and charge in US dollars (USD) with Stripe as our payment service provider.
How does billing work?
Billing is determined by calculating the number of searches performed and documents stored during a billing cycle. If you exceed the number of searches or documents included in a billing cycle for a dedicated plan, you will be charged for additional usage at $0.20 per 1,000 searches and $0.05 per 1,000 documents. How do you calculate the number of documents?
The number of documents calculated for billing purposes is the total number of documents across all indexes in a project at the time of billing.
Can I change my plan?
Yes, and it is effortless. You can change a project’s plan by selecting the project, navigating to the “Billing” tab, and clicking on the “Change plan” button. Please remember that once the process is complete, you won’t be able to downgrade your plan.
What are the support hours?
Standard support hours are 8AM-5PM CET. Support operates from Monday to Thursday.
