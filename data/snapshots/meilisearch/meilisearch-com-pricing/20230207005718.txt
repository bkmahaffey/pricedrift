FAQs
Can I try Meilisearch cloud for free?
Yes. We offer a free plan that allows you to index up to 100k documents and perform 10k search requests per month. If you exceed this usage you’ll be charged 25c for each additional block of 1,000 searches and 1,000 documents.
Is there a minimum term or commitment once I start paying?
There is no minimum term, and users can close their accounts anytime. Users will only be charged for the period during which a project was active. That said, if you’re unhappy with Meilisearch, we’d like to know why. It’s important to us that our users are happy. Please do not hesitate to contact us through the chat widget or email us at cloud@meilisearch.com
and provide a detailed explanation of what happened. We’ll work with you to ensure you have the best possible experience. To delete your account, navigate to the account settings on your dashboard and click the “Delete your account” button.
How do we accept payment?
We only accept payment online via credit cards and charge in US dollars (USD) with Stripe as our payment service provider.
Can I change my plan?
Yes, and it is effortless. You can upgrade a project’s plan by selecting the project, navigating to the "Billing" tab, and clicking on the "Change plan" button. Please remember that once the process is complete, you won’t be able to downgrade your plan.
