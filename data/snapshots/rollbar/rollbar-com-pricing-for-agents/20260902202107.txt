Rollbar pricing
This page is a machine-readable summary of Rollbar’s pricing, published for AI agents and automated buyers. Rollbar has 4 plans: Free, Essentials, Advanced, and Enterprise. Essentials and Advanced are usage-based: the price scales in steps with monthly error occurrences, from 10,000 to 50,000,000 per month. Every price step is listed in the tables below.
All plans include unlimited projects, unlimited users, unlimited service links, unlimited custom fields and tags, a REST API and Terraform provider, and SDKs for server, client, and mobile platforms. Rollbar never charges per seat.
Prefer the interactive calculator? See rollbar.com/pricing.
Plans
Free: $0/month forever. Includes 5,000 occurrences/month, 1,000 session replays/month, 30-day data retention, and unlimited users. No credit card required.
Essentials: from $9/month at 10,000 occurrences/month up to $6,499/month at 50,000,000 occurrences/month. 90-day data retention, fine-grained rate limiting, and 4,000 AI credits/month included. Rollbar Analyze (RQL + Metrics API) is a paid add-on on this plan.
Advanced: from $13/month at 10,000 occurrences/month up to $12,049/month at 50,000,000 occurrences/month. 180-day data retention, adaptive alerts, SCIM, 8,000 AI credits/month included, and Rollbar Analyze (RQL + Metrics API) included natively.
Enterprise: custom pricing for teams over 80,000,000 events/month. Custom retention, custom BAA for HIPAA, audit controls, a dedicated customer success manager, and custom SLAs. Contact sales via rollbar.com/contact-us.

Overage
Rollbar does not offer unlimited or flat-rate overage. When a plan’s occurrence limit is reached, one of three behaviors applies, configurable per account:
Stop processing new events until the next billing cycle. This is the default: cost stays predictable, but new events are dropped.
On-demand events billed at the plan’s same flat per-event rate. There is no premium overage rate.
A dollar-capped overage budget that you set in advance.
Billing
Monthly and annual billing are both available. Annual billing is discounted: effectively 2 months free compared with paying monthly. Prices are in USD.
FAQ
Does Rollbar charge per seat?
No. All plans include unlimited users.
How many plans does Rollbar have?
Four: Free, Essentials, Advanced, and Enterprise (custom pricing, for teams over 80,000,000 events/month).
What happens if I go over my occurrence limit?
See Overage above. Rollbar never bills an unbounded amount; you choose stop-processing, same-rate on-demand events, or a capped overage budget.
Is there a free plan?
Yes: 5,000 occurrences/month and 1,000 session replays/month, unlimited users, no credit card required, free forever.
Where can I see the exact price for my volume?
Every price step is listed in the tables on this page. rollbar.com/pricing has an interactive calculator. For Enterprise volumes (80M+ events/month), contact sales.
