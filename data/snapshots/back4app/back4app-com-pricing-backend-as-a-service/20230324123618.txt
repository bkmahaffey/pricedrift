FAQ - Frequently Asked
Questions
What are the advantages of Back4app vs Firebase?
The reasons you will enjoy using Back4App over Firebase are: We ’ re open source
No vendor lock-in
You get predictable monthly pricing
Relational data models
Performatic heavy transactional-queries
We offer dedicated hosting
Enterprise plans for larger customers
To know more about the benefits of using Back4App, check out our article Why use Back4app.
How do I monitor usage?
You can monitor your usage through our dashboard. Once you log in, the option to monitor usage is right there. To learn more, click here.
What is the price per extra request?
MVP Plan - Once you reach your limit, extra requests cost $5 per every 100k requests.
Pay as you go plan - Once you reach your limit, extra requests cost $2 per every 100k requests.
What is the price per extra data storage?
Once you reach your limit, extra requests cost $15 per Gigabyte per month.
What is the price per extra file storage?
Once you reach your limit, extra requests cost $1 per 10 Gigabytes stored per month.
What is the price per extra data transfer?
Once you reach your limit, extra requests cost $0.1 per Gigabyte.
Can I test Back4app without being billed?
You sure can! Our free plan is the perfect way to experiment with Back4App before committing to a paid plan.
How many apps can I run on the Dedicated Plan?
Our dedicated plan supports up to five applications. If you need a customized plan to run more apps, contact us
We’re happy to help set something up that works for your specific needs.
Do you charge per app?
Yes, we do. The exception is the Dedicated Plan that supports up to five apps on each subscription.
How to cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription any time. All you need to do is fill out a cancellation form on your account.
What type of support will I receive?
The kind of support you receive depends on your plan. The chart above tells you exactly what level of support you can expect from each plan.
Is there a cap on the number of projects I have on the free tier?
You can have up to five projects on the free tier.
How do I upgrade / downgrade my plans?
Log in and go to the plan usage section of the dashboard. From there, click upgrade and select the plan you wish to move to. You can downgrade this way, as well. To learn more, click here.
Where to find information about the terms of service, Privacy Policy, and Copyright?
Our Terms of Service
Privacy Policy
and Copyright Policy
can be found by following the appropriate link. They’re also found in the footer of our website.
