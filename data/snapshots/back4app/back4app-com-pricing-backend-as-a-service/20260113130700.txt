FAQ - Frequently Asked
Questions
What are the advantages of Back4app vs Firebase?
The reasons you will enjoy using Back4app over Firebase are: We run on top of open-source technologies
No vendor lock-in
You get predictable monthly pricing
Relational data models
Performative heavy transactional-queries
We offer dedicated hosting
Enterprise plans for larger customers
To know more about the benefits of using Back4app, check out our article Why use Back4app.
How do I monitor usage?
Easily track your usage via our dashboard. Upon login, immediately access the usage monitoring feature. For further details, click here.
What is the price per extra data storage?
Upon crossing your storage threshold, you'll be subject to a $15 monthly fee for each additional gigabyte of data storage required.
What is the price per extra request?
MVP Plan: After exceeding your quota, additional requests are billed at $5 per 100 K requests.
Pay-as-you-go Plan: After exceeding your quota, additional requests are billed at $2 per 100 K requests.
What is the price per extra file storage?
After exceeding your quota, additional storage is billed at $1 per each 10 gigabytes of storage.
What is the price per extra data transfer?
After exceeding your quota, additional data transfer is billed at $0.1 per each 1 gigabyte of transfer.
Can I test Back4app without being billed?
You sure can! Our free plan is the perfect way to experiment with Back4app before committing to a paid plan.
How many apps can I run on the Dedicated Plan?
Our dedicated plan supports one application. If you need a customized plan to run more apps, contact us
We're happy to help set something up that works for your specific needs.
Does the Dedicated Plan offer unlimited requests?
The Dedicated Plan operates on a fixed resource structure, a cluster with 10 CPUs / 14 GB, processing as many requests as the hardware can handle. However, it's important to emphasize that it doesn't imply unlimited capacity and does not mean it can support any workload, regardless of size and volume.
Are the thresholds on the Dedicated Plan per app or plan?
The thresholds specified on the Dedicated Plan apply to the cluster and not to each individual application. For instance, the total file storage will be 1 TB for all applications, not for each one.
Do you charge per app?
Yes, we do. If you need a customized plan to run more apps, contact us
We're happy to help set something up that works for your specific needs.
How to cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription any time. All you need to do is fill out a cancellation form on your account.
What type of support will I receive?
The kind of support you receive depends on your plan. The chart above tells you exactly what level of support you can expect from each plan.
Is there a cap on the number of projects I have on the free tier?
You can have up to five projects on the free tier.
How do I upgrade / downgrade my plans?
Log in and go to the plan usage section of the dashboard. From there, click upgrade and select the plan you wish to move to. You can downgrade this way, as well. To learn more, click here.
