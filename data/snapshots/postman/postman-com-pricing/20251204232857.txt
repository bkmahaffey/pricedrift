Frequently asked questions
What is Postman?
Postman is the leading API platform used by over 40 million developers and 500,000 companies. It helps teams design, test, and manage APIs in one collaborative workspace. With built-in tools for automation, documentation, and governance, Postman makes API development faster, more reliable, and more secure.
How is Postman priced?
Postman uses per-user, per-month pricing. You pay for each team member with workspace access, billed monthly or annually. Save ~25% with annual billing.
Can I change the number of licenses during my subscription?
Yes. All paid plans let you add or adjust licenses as your team grows.
Basic or Professional plans: You can add new team members anytime directly from your management console. With our Auto-Flex policy
you won't be charged immediately - instead:
Monthly plans: New users are billed at your next monthly renewal. You can add or remove users throughout the month, and you'll only be charged for the final count at renewal.
Annual plans: New users are billed quarterly. Between billing cycles, you can freely adjust your user count up or down, and you'll only pay for the net change when the quarterly billing occurs.
Enterprise plans: Contact sales@postman.com
to add licenses or adjust your team size. We'll work with you to accommodate your needs.
This gives you flexibility to scale your team without worrying about mid-cycle charges. Learn more in Postman's Learning Centre
.
Can I try Enterprise features before buying?
Yes! We offer a 30-day Enterprise trial (called Enterprise+ in-product) so you can experience advanced collaboration and automation features before upgrading.
Who can access the trial? Any team on the Free plan with 3 or fewer users. The trial automatically starts when you invite or approve a 4th team member. Learn more here
Can my company submit a purchase order or receive an invoice?
Yes. The Enterprise plan supports purchase orders, invoicing, and custom payment terms. The Free, Basic, and Professional plans are available by credit card only.
What happens when I exceed my limits?
Each Postman plan includes monthly resource limits for things like mock server requests, monitor runs, collection runs, and API calls. When you approach or exceed these limits, Postman will notify you before your work is interrupted.
Here's how it works:
Soft limits: You'll receive in-app and email alerts as you near your usage threshold. You can upgrade your plan or purchase add-ons to increase capacity.
Hard limits: Once a hard limit is reached, that specific feature (for example, mock server requests or monitor runs) will pause until your next billing cycle or until you upgrade.
Plan upgrades and add-ons: You can view your team's resource usage anytime in the Resource Usage dashboard. If you need more capacity, you can enable pay-as-you-go, purchase add-ons, or upgrade your plan.
AI credits: Postman AI has its own monthly credit limits per user
Credits reset each month and can be topped up through pay-as-you-go pricing.
Tip: Reaching your limits frequently is a good sign your team is ready for a higher plan. Enterprise teams can contact their Postman representative to customize usage limits.

Companies of every size use Postman - from startups building their first APIs to global enterprises running thousands of them (internally or publicly). Postman powers industries like finance, telecom, healthcare, SaaS, and AI by making API development faster, more consistent, and more secure.

Is Postman secure?
Yes. Postman follows enterprise-grade security and compliance standards and invests heavily in protecting your data. You can learn more about certifications, encryption, secret management, and data practices on the Postman Security Portal
.

Add-ons expand your plan with advanced capabilities that help your team automate, secure, and scale your API workflows.
Examples include:
Postman AI for natural language-powered API workflows.
Flows for visual API workflows and AI-powered logic
Collection Runner for automated and large-scale test execution across collections

You can purchase them here on the Postman Pricing Page.
What is included in the Advanced Security Administration add-on?
The Advanced Security Administration add-on provides enterprise-wide, robust security capabilities that help safeguard your sensitive data and intellectual property across all Postman workspaces. With advanced encryption, secret management, and seamless integrations, it ensures your API development remains secure at every stage.
This add-on includes:
Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Maintain full control over your data encryption by managing your own encryption keys.
Private Secret Scanner: Detect exposed secrets across both Team and Public Workspaces using Postman's built-in scanning tool.
Secret Scanner Dashboard: Access detailed analytics and reports on detected secrets to monitor and manage exposure risks.
Vault Integrations: Connect Postman with your preferred secret management solutions — Azure Key Vault, HashiCorp Vault, or AWS Secrets Manager — to securely fetch secrets at runtime.
Domain Capture: Automatically route Postman logins using corporate email addresses to your company's Enterprise account for centralized access control.
What is a Flows credit?
A flow is made up of building blocks
such as Action blocks for calling APIs, Decision blocks for computation, and AI blocks which can generate JSON, text, or images. Every block consumes a set number of credits when flows run. An AI block consumes a variable number of credits depending on how the block is configured and your subscription plan.
How do AI credits work?
Each plan includes AI credits
that reset monthly. Credits are used when you generate tests, create collections, or automate documentation. Free plan users can upgrade once credits run out.
How is Postman AI different from Postbot?
Postman AI is the next generation of Postman's AI experience, offering broader capabilities and credit-based usage. Postbot remains available for Enterprise customers until Postman AI is Enterprise-ready.
