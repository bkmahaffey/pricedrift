Frequently asked questions
What is the top tier of customer support offered by Postman?
The Postman Enterprise plan offers our top-tier support package, with access to our support specialists and a one-day response time. Customers on the Enterprise plan are also assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager.
Will developers who are not working with a team (e.g., just using Postman for personal projects) need to start paying for Postman?
No, developers not working with a team are not required to pay for Postman. We love our community of global Postman users, and we will continue to offer the free version of Postman. The free version includes many features you know and love, including sending requests and inspecting responses, designing and mocking APIs, testing APIs, monitoring APIs, and much more. The free version is also a great place to start collaborating with up to three team members.
Can my company submit a purchase order? Can you invoice my company?
The Postman Enterprise plan offers PO/invoicing as a payment option. The Enterprise plan also offers custom payment terms. The Postman Basic and Professional plans are only available for purchase by credit card.
Can I increase licenses during my subscription?
Yes. For customers on the Postman Basic and Professional plans, you can add licenses at any point in the subscription.
If you're on the Basic or Professional plan, you can simply go to your management console to add a new member. With our Auto-Flex team policy
you won't be billed for the new users until either your next billing cycle for monthly plans or on a recurring quarterly basis for annual plans. During this monthly or quarterly period, you will also have the ability to assess your needs and add more licenses or remove them before the billing date. For additional information, please refer to this article
in the Postman Learning Center.
If you're on the Enterprise plan, contact us at sales@postman.com
and we'll help you add more licenses.
Can I decrease licenses during my subscription?
Yes. For monthly plans, a request to decrease the license seat count will be reflected in the following month's billing. For annual plans, a request to decrease license seats occurs at the next annual renewal.
What standard security questionnaires and reports are available from Postman?
Postman recognizes that our customers and prospects will require security-related information to adhere to their own organizational compliance policy requirements. Therefore, you can freely view Postman's SOC 3 Report
and we have completed the following standard security questionnaires and reports, which requires an NDA and can be requested by contacting sales@postman.com
.
SOC 2 Type II
CAIQ - Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire
We also provide additional information about security
and our privacy policy
on the Postman website.

Postman Flows access and pricing
How do I access Flows?
Flows is currently available on our Free and annual Professional plans. It will eventually be available on our Enterprise plan.
Our Free plan allows for up to three Flows users
Access is available as a $20 per person add-on, per month, in our annual Professional plan
Add users by assigning them the team role of Flow Editor
Are there any limits to using Flows?
When executed locally, Flows can run an unlimited number of times
Limits apply to how many requests can be sent when deployed Flows are executed on Postman Cloud: Free plan: 5,000 Flow requests/month
Professional plan: 100,000 requests/month
Each plan includes a set amount of payload data for executing Flows on Postman Cloud: 10MB per execution for Free plan
100MB per execution for Professional plan
A single Flow can run for up to 30 minutes
Which versions of Postman support Flows?
Postman Flows is available only on Postman v10.12.5 and later
Flows created during open beta will no longer work on Postman v9.x
Update to the latest version of Postman here
What if I upgrade from Free to the Basic plan?
Flows is unavailable on the Basic plan, so any Flows your team created will become inaccessible
Any deployed Flows will stop executing
If you require greater usage than the Free plan allows, we recommend upgrading to Professional
What if I upgrade to the Enterprise plan?
Because Flows is not yet available on Enterprise plans, any Flows your team created will become inaccessible
Any deployed Flows will stop executing
We'll be offering Flows on Enterprise plans soon; stay tuned!
I created Flows as part of your Early Access program, and I'm on a paid plan. What will happen to my Flows access?
Thanks for trying out Flows and sharing your feedback! The creators of the Flows in your team can continue using Flows for free until your plan renews. Here's what's included:
If you're on the Basic or Professional plan, you'll have 10,000 Flow requests/month and 10MB per payload execution on the Postman Cloud until your plan renews
If you're on the Enterprise plan, you'll have 50,000 Flow requests/month and 50MB per payload execution on the Postman Cloud until your plan renews
When will billing start for Flows?
Billing started May 15, 2023
Flows can be purchased by buying add-on licenses for $20 per user, per month, billed annually on our Professional plan
