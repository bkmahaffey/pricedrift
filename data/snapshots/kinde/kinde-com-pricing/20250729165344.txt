FAQ
How does Kinde's pricing work?
You only pay for what you need. Every plan includes a free tier of 10,500 monthly active users (MAU), with paid features and usage billed as you scale.
Do paid customers count toward my MAU limit?
No, users on your paid subscription plans do not count toward your MAU allowance. You’re never charged twice for the same user.
Are there any setup or hidden fees?
No. Kinde does not charge any setup fees or hidden charges. All pricing is transparent.
Is the Free plan really free?
Yes, our Free plan gives you 10,500 monthly active users at no cost, and does not require a credit card to get started.
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan at any time?
Yes. You can change or cancel your plan whenever you like.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, and offer invoicing for larger plans and enterprise customers.
Do you offer annual billing or discounts?
Yes, annual billing and discounts are available. Contact sales for more details.
How is billing handled for usage above plan limits?
If you exceed your included limits, overages are billed automatically at transparent, published rates.
Does Kinde support authentication for AI?
Kinde has full support for machine-to-machine tokens (M2M) as well as extensive support for OAuth 2.1.
What does Kinde's billing module do?
Kinde’s billing module lets you create and manage subscription plans, usage-based pricing, and customer invoices—all in one place, fully integrated with authentication and feature flags.
Can I use Kinde billing without using authentication?
Yes, you can use Kinde’s billing module independently, or together with our authentication and feature management features.
Does Kinde billing support usage-based and feature-based pricing?
Yes, Kinde supports metered billing, feature-based entitlements, and flexible plan configuration for SaaS products.
Can my customers upgrade, downgrade, or cancel their own plans?
Yes, Kinde includes a customer self-serve portal for managing subscriptions and billing.
How does Kinde billing compare to standalone billing tools?
Kinde billing is built for SaaS from day one with fully integrated with user management and feature access, so you don't have to sync data or stitch together different platforms.
Can I add premium support to my plan?
Yes, Kinde Care premium support is available as an add-on to any plan, and is included in Enterprise packages.
What compliance regimes and certifications does Kinde have?
Kinde is compiant with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR (with DPA), HIPAA, CAIQ v4, MVSP and PCI-DSS.
Are enterprise and compliance features available on all plans?
Enterprise features like advanced security, SSO, audit logs, and compliance reporting are available on higher-tier plans.
Do you charge extra for feature flags or other modules?
No. Feature flags, custom roles, and core platform tools are included in all paid plans.
Do you charge for inactive or test users?
No, Kinde only charges for active users. Inactive and test users do not count toward your billable usage.
