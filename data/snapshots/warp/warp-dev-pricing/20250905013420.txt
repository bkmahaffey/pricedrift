Frequently Asked Questions
Does Warp have a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation?
Yes, Warp has obtained a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation from an accredited third party. Visit Warp’s Trust Center
to request the report.
Should I subscribe to a Turbo Team or choose the Business plan?
If you’re an individual or part of a small team looking for powerful AI features, the Turbo plan is an excellent choice. It offers the most generous AI limits, individually configurable Zero Data Retention controls, and collaboration tools such as shared Notebooks, Workflows, and real-time Session Sharing.
If you’re part of a growing team (up to 50 people) and require more advanced controls, the Business plan is the better option. It includes everything in Turbo, plus SAML-based SSO, automatically enforced team-wide Zero Data Retention, and centralized billing — ideal for organizations that need stronger security, enhanced administrative capabilities, and compliance support.
How can I subscribe to a Warp Business or Enterprise plan?
You can sign up for the Business plan directly from our pricing page ("Start today") or via our upgrade page
.
Warp also offers an Enterprise plan with custom pricing for larger engineering organizations or businesses with advanced security and compliance requirements. If you’d like to explore whether an Enterprise plan might be the best fit for your team, contact us at [email protected]
or submit a request at warp.dev/contact-sales
to start the conversation.
Does Warp have Zero Data Retention policies with LLM providers?
Warp integrates with multiple Large Language Model (LLM) providers to power its AI-driven features.
These providers include, but are not limited to:
Anthropic
OpenAI
Google
Fireworks AI
Warp has executed Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreements with these providers. This means that, by default across all plans:
LLM providers commit not to train their models on any customer-generated data processed through Warp’s services.
LLM providers commit to delete inputs and outputs after generating the relevant output, within a fixed time period.
Warp enforces these commitments through both technical measures and contractual safeguards with the LLM providers.
How can I enable Zero Data Retention in Warp?
Zero Data Retention (ZDR) can be enabled in two ways:
Individual level – Any user can enable full ZDR for their own account by disabling Help Improve Warp in their Warp settings under Privacy.
Organization-wide – On the Business and Enterprise plans, admins can enforce ZDR for all members by default, ensuring team-wide compliance without relying on individual settings.
If you’d like to request organization-wide ZDR, please contact your Warp sales representative at [email protected]
or submit a request via our Contact Sales form
.
Regardless of plan, Warp never allows OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other model providers to store, retain, or train their models on your data. Warp has full Zero Data Retention policies with all LLM providers.
Do all paid plans support pay-as-you-go overages?
Yes! Pro, Turbo, Business, and Lightspeed all support pay-as-you-go AI overages. This means you can continue using premium models even after hitting your quota – just enable overages under Settings > Billing and usage.
How often do AI request limits reset?
Allotted AI requests refill every 30 days from your signup date. You can follow along with your refill period by referencing the counter under Settings > Billing and usage.
What payment options are available?
Warp uses Stripe for payment processing and the only available payment method is by credit card.
Warp cannot currently accept payment by ACH, cash, check, money order, or crypto-currency.
Are there any Warp discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams?
At this time, Warp does not offer any discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams. We recommend checking out Warp’s Free plan which includes all of the core terminal features and enough AI usage to help you get started.
I am an individual developer and I need more than 2,500 AI requests per month. What's the right plan for me?
If you need more than 2,500 AI requests per month, the Warp Turbo plan includes 10,000 AI requests per month, offering strong performance and flexibility for heavier AI usage.
For even higher throughput, the Warp Lightspeed plan includes 50,000 AI requests per month, built for advanced power users and teams running intensive agentic workflows across large codebases.
Pro, Turbo, and Lightspeed all support pay-as-you-go overages, so you can continue using premium models even after hitting your monthly quota. Team admins can enable overages at any time under Settings > Billing and usage.
While Turbo and Lightspeed are great for individuals, they also include powerful collaboration features for teams like unlimited shared Notebooks and Workflows via Warp Drive, plus real-time session sharing to help teams move faster together.
I am an individual developer interested in a self-service plan. Can I bring my own LLM API key?
At the moment, Warp only supports Bring Your Own LLM on the Enterprise plan. This is because Warp wants to maintain the quality of the model for terminal use cases. For Enterprise customers, we can work together closely to ensure the quality and compliance of the models.
