Frequently Asked Questions
Are there annual discounts available for Warp's Turbo Plan?
Yes. If you subscribe to Warp Turbo for a full year, you’ll receive a discounted rate of $40 per user per month, compared to $50 per user per month when billed monthly.
Teams of three or more get an even bigger discount, with pricing at $30 per user per month when billed annually or $35 per user per month when billed monthly.
Are there any Warp discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams?
At this time, Warp does not offer any discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams. We recommend checking out Warp’s Free plan which includes all of the core terminal features and enough AI usage to help you get started.
What payment options are available?
Warp uses Stripe for payment processing and the only available payment method is by credit card.
Warp cannot currently accept payment by ACH, cash, check, money order, or crypto-currency.
How often do AI request limits reset?
Allotted AI requests refill every 30 days from your signup date. You can follow along with your refill period by referencing the counter under Settings > AI. When you upgrade to a Team plan, you will be given unlimited requests immediately.
How can I subscribe to a Warp Enterprise plan?
Warp offers an Enterprise plan with custom pricing for larger engineering organizations or businesses with advanced security and compliance requirements. If you are interested in learning whether an Enterprise plan might be the best fit for your team, please contact us
to kick off a conversation.
I am an individual developer interested in a self-service plan. Can I bring my own LLM API key?
At the moment, Warp only supports Bring Your Own LLM on the Enterprise plan. This is because Warp wants to maintain quality of the model for terminal use cases. For Enterprise customers, we can work together closely to ensure the quality and compliance of the models.
I am an individual developer and I need more than 2,500 AI requests per month. What's the right plan for me?
If you need more than 2,500 AI requests per month, the Warp Turbo plan is the best option. Turbo includes 10,000 AI requests per month plus unlimited access to Lite models, giving you plenty of power and flexibility for heavy AI usage.
Both Pro and Turbo also support pay-as-you-go overages, so you can keep using premium AI models even after hitting your monthly quota. Team admins can enable overages at any time under Settings > Billing and usage.
While Turbo is great for individual developers, it also includes powerful collaboration features like unlimited shared Notebooks and Workflows via Warp Drive, plus real-time session sharing to help teams work faster together.
Does Warp have a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation?
Yes, Warp has obtained a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation from an accredited third party. Visit Warp’s Trust Center
to request the report.
