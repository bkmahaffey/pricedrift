Agents

Agent Management UX
Run multiple agents in parallel, track their progress on complex tasks, and step in easily when they need help.
Agent Profiles, Permissions, and Autonomy
Customize how your Agent behaves—from its models and autonomy to the tools and permissions for reading, writing, and executing commands & code.
Model Choice
