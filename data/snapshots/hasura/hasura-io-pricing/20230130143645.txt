Does Hasura do database hosting?
No, Hasura extends your existing databases wherever it is hosted and provides an instant GraphQL API that can be securely accessed for web, mobile, and data integration workloads.

What is data pass-through?
Hasura Cloud meters data passing through it to external services, including request data that is sent to upstream services (eg: databases) and response data sent to downstream services (eg: web apps). You will be billed based on this volume of data that passes through Hasura every month.
What happens if I upgrade to the Standard Tier in the middle of a month?
If you upgrade to the Standard Tier in the middle of a (calendar) month, and: Your project’s usage after you upgrade is less than 20GiB for that month: You'll only be charged for the number of days your project was on the Standard Tier. Eg: if you upgrade on the 11th of September and are on the Standard Tier from September 11 to September 30, using 15 GiB, you'll be charged $66 for 20 days in September.
Your project’s usage after you upgrade is more than 20GiB for that month: You will be charged the full 99$, plus 2$ for every additional GiB over 20 GiB.
What happens when I downgrade to the Free Tier from the Standard Tier?
You can downgrade to the Free Tier anytime. If your project’s usage is less than 20GiB for that calendar month, you will only be charged for the number of days your project was on the Standard Tier. Eg: In September, if you were on the Standard Tier from September 1 to 20, and your usage was 15 GiB, you will only be billed $66 for 20 days in September.
If your project’s usage is above 20GiB for that calendar month, you'll be charged the full 99$ plus 2$ for every additional GiB over 20 GiB, irrespective of the number of days spent on the Standard Tier.
