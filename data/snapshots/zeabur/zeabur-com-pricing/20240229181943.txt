Usage
Besides the subscription fee, Zeabur charges for actual hardware resource usage.
CPU
$0.0003 / vCPU per minute
about $12 / vCPU per month
Memory
$0.0001 / GB per minute
about $4 / GB per month
Network Egress
$0.1 / GB
Persistent Volume
$0.2 / GB per month
Usage is billed in minute-level granularity, so you only pay for what you use.
