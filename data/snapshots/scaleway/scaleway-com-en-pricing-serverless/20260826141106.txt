Understanding the difference between execution and provisioning:
Resource consumption (Active execution): You are only billed for the exact duration your function is running to process a request. When it stops executing, billing stops.
Provisioned resources (Warm standby): To mitigate cold start latency, you can choose to keep function instances permanently awake (by setting a minimum scale value). These instances are billed continuously for as long as they are kept warm, whether they are actively processing requests or idle.
