Does Axiom offer a free plan?
Yes, Axiom Cloud offers a Personal plan with no charge. This includes an "Always Free" allowance of 500 GB of data loading per month, 10 GB-hours of query compute per month, and 25 GB of storage. This is ideal for hobbyists or small-scale usage.
What are Axiom's deployment options?
Axiom Cloud is a fully managed service with usage-based pricing.
How is Axiom's pricing structured?
Axiom's pricing has four main components:
Platform fee: A base monthly charge for using Axiom Cloud
Compute: Charges for data loading and query operations (measured in compute credits)
Storage: Charges for the compressed data you store
Add-ons: Optional enterprise features available as additional purchases
What happens if I exceed my included usage limits?
For Axiom Cloud deployments, usage beyond your included allowances is billed at the rates specified in the pricing table. Axiom provides usage dashboards, alerts, and caps to help you monitor and control your spending.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, with Axiom Cloud you can adjust your usage and add-ons at any time.
What regions are available for Axiom Cloud deployments?
Axiom Cloud offers deployments in both US and EU regions.
What are compute credits?
Compute credits are Axiom's consistent unit of measurement for compute resources. They apply to various operations including data loading and querying. You can purchase compute credits on demand or pre-purchase them at a discount.
How does data loading compute work?
When you send data to Axiom, it's processed and optimized. This processing is measured in compute credits based on the volume of data loaded. Rates decrease as your volume grows via automatic volume discounts.
How does query compute work?
Queries in Axiom consume resources based on the memory allocated and the duration of the query. Axiom multiplies memory (in GB) by duration (in hours) to calculate usage. As with data loading, higher volumes qualify for reduced rates via automatic discounts.
How does storage pricing work?
Axiom charges for storage based on the compressed size of your data, measured in gigabytes per month. Thanks to Axiom's industry-leading compression, customers typically enjoy a 95% reduction in storage footprint.
What is the "Always Free" allowance?
The "Always Free" allowance for Axiom Cloud includes:
1,000 GB/month of data loading compute
100 GB-hours/month of query compute
100 GB/month of storage
The Personal plan includes a smaller allowance of 500 GB/month of data loading, 10 GB-hours/month of query compute, and 25 GB/month of storage.
Do pre-purchased compute credits expire?
No, pre-purchased compute credits do not expire as long as you remain an active Axiom customer. This policy ensures fairness if your usage patterns change or if you've pre-purchased more credits than initially needed. Credits remain associated with your organization and can be used at any time during your active subscription. However, credits cannot be transferred or refunded, and would be forfeited if your Axiom organization is terminated.
What enterprise features are available as add-ons?
For Axiom Cloud, the following enterprise features are available as add-ons:
AML Single Sign-On (SSO): $100/month
Directory Sync: $100/month
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): $50/month
Audit Log: $50/month
Can I access SOC-2 and HIPAA compliance reports?
Yes, with an NDA and a minimum annual spend on Axiom Cloud.
How can I monitor and control my Axiom costs?
Axiom provides several tools to help you manage costs:
Real-time usage information in your organization settings
Custom alerts and notifications through Axiom's audit log dataset
Usage caps and controls
Billing thresholds that trigger invoices before the end of a billing cycle
What is the Compute Credit Pre-Purchase Program?
This program allows you to pre-purchase compute credits at a discount based on volume. Axiom Cloud includes automatic volume discounts—this program allows your organization to receive additional discounts for commitment. Pre-purchased credits do not expire and offer significant savings for predictable workloads:
25,000-99,999 credits: $0.90 per credit (10% discount)
100,000-249,999 credits: $0.85 per credit (15% discount)
250,000-499,999 credits: $0.80 per credit (20% discount)
500,000-999,999 credits: $0.75 per credit (25% discount)
1,000,000+ credits: $0.70 per credit (30% discount)
What are billing thresholds?
Billing thresholds trigger invoices when your accrued usage reaches a specific monetary amount within a monthly billing cycle. For Axiom Cloud, this threshold is $1,000. This helps prevent unexpected costs at the end of the month.
Are there limits to the number of datasets, users, or monitors I can create?
Yes, Axiom has soft limits that can be increased upon request:
1,000 users, 100 datasets, 1,024 fields per dataset, 500 monitors
What is the maximum retention period for my data?
Retention is configurable based on your needs. You're billed for the actual storage consumed, so longer retention periods will increase your storage costs proportionally.
What support options are available?
All Axiom customers receive email support. Additional support options available for purchase include:
Named support engineer
Dedicated support engineer
Training and professional services
Does Axiom offer professional services?
Yes, Axiom offers professional services to help with implementation, optimization, and training. These services are available at an additional cost.
