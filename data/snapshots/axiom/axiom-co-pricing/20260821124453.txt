SPEND AND OVERAGES
How does the Always Free allowance work? What's the catch?
No catch. Start on Personal with no credit card (500 GB load / 10 GB-hours / 25 GB storage) or get more headroom on Axiom Cloud (1 TB load / 100 GB-hours / 100 GB storage, included in the $25/mo platform fee). You only pay when you exceed the free limits.
What happens when usage exceeds the Always Free allowance?
Pay-as-you-go kicks in automatically on Axiom Cloud. Per-unit rates drop at higher volume tiers, so total cost grows sub-linearly with usage. No overage SKU.
Can I set a spending limit?
Yes. Spending limits and spend alerts are set in-console; usage beyond the limit is paused rather than auto-billed.
How do volume discounts work?
Automatic and in-console, applied at each volume threshold. No negotiation, no sales call.
PLAN FIT
What's the difference between Personal and Axiom Cloud?
Personal is for individual developers and small projects (no credit card, smaller free allowance, 30-day retention). Axiom Cloud is for teams ($25/mo platform fee, larger free allowance, configurable retention, self-serve enterprise add-ons). Same architecture across both.
Can I pre-buy credits, or is everything pay-as-you-go?
Both. Pay-as-you-go is the default; pre-purchase credits whenever you want deeper discounts (up to 30% off on top of automatic volume discounts; credits never expire).
Can I self-serve enterprise add-ons (SSO, RBAC, audit logs) without a sales call?
Yes. Available in-console at any plan level once you're on Axiom Cloud.
CUSTOM AND PROCUREMENT
Do you support AWS Marketplace billing?
Yes.
Can I get invoice or PO-based billing?
Yes, on Axiom Cloud with pre-purchased compute credits. Same product, just with a credit commitment that earns deeper discounts.
Do you offer migration and services support?
Yes. 4Data in EMEA (including deep Splunk-migration expertise), plus US-based services support directly from Axiom.
SPECIFIC FEATURES
Are MCP server access and the SRE skill priced separately?
No. MCP server, SRE skill, and Metrics skill are included on Axiom Cloud at no extra cost. No separate rate limits on agent use either; agent queries count toward your query-hour usage the same way human queries do.
What's the data-residency story?
Edge architecture supports data residency and write-side multi-region from a single Axiom organization. It doesn't automatically fail over query reads; if a region is down, queries against that region pause until it recovers.
