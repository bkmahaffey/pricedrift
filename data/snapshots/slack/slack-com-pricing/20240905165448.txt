Frequently asked questions
How do I purchase Slack AI?
Slack AI is available to purchase as an add-on for all paid plans. Here’s how to buy Slack AI:
If you haven’t signed up for Slack, create a workspace
to get started. You can upgrade your workspace
and purchase the Slack AI add-on at the same time. To do so, select a paid plan and then click With Slack AI.
If you’re on the Pro or Business+ plan, sign in to your account and click Get Slack AI on the Pricing page
.
On Enterprise Grid? Reach out to your Slack account executive or contact our Sales team
.
How does Slack AI work?
Slack AI
uses the discussions and information already in Slack to power a secure experience tailored to you and your organization:
Summarize long conversations
Find answers to questions
Get a daily recap of less important channels
How does Slack AI protect the security of my data?
Slack AI runs on Slack’s trusted infrastructure, and upholds the security practices and compliance standards
that customers expect from Slack itself. Slack does not share customer data with large language model (LLM) providers and does not use customer data to train LLMs. Visit our Help Center for more details
.
Our large language models are hosted directly within Slack’s AWS virtual private cloud, ensuring your data remains in-house.
How can I pay for Slack? Can I pay by credit card, or can you invoice me?
You can use a credit card to pay for any plan. If you choose to pay annually, you may be eligible to be invoiced and pay by ACH or wire transfer. Visit our Help Center
for more details, or contact us to start the process.
Our team has several accounts which are not being actively used, but which we don’t want to disable. Do we still have to pay for them?
At Slack, we believe you should only be billed for what you use. Members who take an action in Slack at any time within a 28-day period will be considered active for billing purposes. If someone you’ve already paid for becomes inactive, we’ll add a prorated credit to your account for the unused time.
We want to add new users to our workspace. How will that be billed?
You’ll be charged a prorated amount for new team members for the remainder of the current billing period.
As an example, suppose your team is on the Pro plan, which costs $8.75 USD per active user per month (when billed monthly). You add a new member 10 days into your billing period with 20 days remaining in the month. The per-day cost for each user is $8.75 divided by 30, so we’ll make a one-time charge of $5.83 ($8.75 divided by 30 days times 20 days) to the credit card on file.
For a detailed overview of our billing model and how your subscription term affects when you will be billed, visit the Help Center
.
Do you offer discounts for non-profit organizations or educational institutions?
We do! Slack wants to support people doing good in the world, which is why we offer special discount pricing for qualified nonprofit organizations
and educational institutions
