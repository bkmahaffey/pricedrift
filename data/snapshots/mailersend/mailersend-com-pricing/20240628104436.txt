Common questions
Is there a trial period?
Yes, there is! Once your account is approved, you can try all the features of the Free plan and send up to 100 emails per month with a Trial plan. You’ll also receive a free trial domain to send up to 100 emails to test out MailerSend before adding a domain.
If you wish to send more emails, and enjoy the full MailerSend experience, you can upgrade to a Premium plan and get 50,000 emails per month.
What are the advantages of using the trial domain?
Adding and verifying your domain involves adding some authentication records to your DNS. If you’re trialing numerous email delivery tools, this can become time-consuming and inconvenient. With the trial domain, you can test out MailerSend to its full potential without adding your own domain, only doing so if you decide it’s the tool for you.
How does the Free plan work?
Once your account is approved, you can subscribe to a Free plan. This plan lets you send up to 3,000 emails per month for free!
Simply go to the Plan and billing page and enter your organization’s address and credit card information to complete your subscription. The billing period starts right after you enter your credit card details.
If you send more than 3,000 emails per month, the cost of additional emails is $1.00 per 1,000 emails. The number of emails that you have sent automatically resets every 30 days.
How does the Premium plan work?
Once your account is approved, you can pick a Premium plan to match the number of emails that you send every month. Save money as you send more emails!
In addition to all the features of the Free plan, you also enjoy unlimited sending domains, templates, users, file uploads, webhooks and API tokens, plus 14 days of log retention, live chat support and a deliverability consultation with an expert.
Subscribe to a Premium plan by choosing a monthly plan on the Plan and billing page. Then enter your organization’s details and credit card information to complete your purchase.
If you exceed the monthly quota of your plan, don’t worry—you can continue sending emails without disruption. These will be charged at an additional low cost per 1,000 emails (applies to all plans). The number of emails that you have sent automatically resets every 30 days.
What if I decide to cancel?
If you no longer wish to use MailerSend, you may cancel at any time. Your subscription will remain active for the remainder of that month's billing cycle, at the end of which you will receive a final charge for any additional emails sent outside of your current plan’s allowance.
Can I use MailerSend with multiple users?
With a premium plan, you can add an unlimited number of users to your account and assign them roles and permissions.
What is the trial domain?
The trial domain is a free domain you can use to conveniently test out MailerSend before adding and verifying your own domain.
What are the limitations of the trial domain?
There is a limit of 100 emails for the trial domain.
Why do you need my credit card details for the Free plan?
As you start increasing your email volume with MailerSend, you might exceed the quota of 3,000 free emails per month included in the Free plan. As additional emails cost $1.00 per 1,000 emails, a payment method is required. Additional emails will be billed during the next billing period.
Can I change my plan later?
You can change your plan at any time! When you upgrade your plan, changes are applied immediately. When you downgrade your plan, changes are applied at the end of your current billing cycle.
What are extra emails?
MailerSend ensures you can keep sending emails if you reach your monthly subscription quota. For example, an $60 subscription includes 100,000 emails. Any extra email sent above that allowance has a cost of $0.80 for a block of 1,000 emails.
What if I hit a limit on my free plan?
Our free plan has a few limits per account: 3 templates, 1 domain, 1 user, 3 API tokens and 1 webhook. To increase your limits, and have the full MailerSend experience, you will need to upgrade to a Premium plan.
