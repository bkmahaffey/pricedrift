Durable Execution
Workflow state survives crashes and deploys. No checkpointing or recovery code.
Built-in failure handling
Automatic retries with backoff, four timeout classes, heartbeating, and saga compensation.
Long-running & scheduled processes
Durable Timers that sleep for months, cron Schedules with backfill, and Continue-As-New.
Live interaction with Workflows
Signals, Queries, and Updates to read and mutate mid-flight, plus Workflow Streams.
Composition across teams
Child Workflows for fault isolation, Temporal Nexus for durable cross-team calls.
Priority & fairness
Native Task Queue priority and fair distribution — no custom queueing layer.
Preview Safe deploys
Worker Versioning pins Workflows to their version; Replay tests validate against real histories.
Runtime control & inspection
Search executions, read Event History and stack traces, reset thousands in one batch operation.
Preview Durable AI agents
OpenAI Agents SDK and Google ADK run LLM and tool calls as durable Activities.
SDKs & developer tooling
