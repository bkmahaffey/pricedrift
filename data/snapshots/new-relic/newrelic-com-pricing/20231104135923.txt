How does New Relic calculate data ingest?
New Relic calculates data ingest costs based on the volume of data your organization saved in the platform after processing. This includes applying data transformation and trimming rules to the raw data in New Relic for more observability value. You can customize the amount of data you send and ingest.

Data retention varies by data option. Data Plus includes up to 90 days of extra retention for most data types over the default. View a comparison table

How much do you charge for egress or data hydration?
Nothing! Unlike our competitors, we don’t charge for data egress or hydration. All your data is indexed and ready to query.

Do you offer volume discounts and commit pricing?
Yes. New Relic offers commitment contract options to Pro and Enterprise edition customers with volume and savings plans that provide volume discounts, predictability, scalability, and flexibility. With the savings plan, you can use an annual or multi-year pool of funds for any combination of usage across the entire term. Plus, New Relic is the only observability vendor that offers an automatic rollover of unused funds for up to 12 months.

Do you offer pay-as-you-go options?
Yes. New Relic offers pay-as-you-go options for Standard and Pro edition customers. You only pay for your monthly usage above the applicable free tier (100 GB ingested). Standard edition customers can simply add a credit card. Contact us
for Pro edition pay-as-you-go options.
How is my monthly bill calculated?
New Relic is one of only two observability vendors that offer usage-based billing. That means we only bill you for your number of provisioned full platform or core users and the amount of GB ingested per month above the free 100 GB. Our original data option is $0.30/GB per month. Our Data Plus option is $0.50/GB per month and includes extended data retention and advanced data governance/performance.
You can also store data in the EU data center for an additional $0.05/GB per month. Additional synthetics checks beyond the amount included in your edition are available at $0.005 per check. If you select our original data option, you can add New Relic Vulnerability Management for $0.10/GB ingested (Vulnerability Management is included with the Data Plus option).
At any point, you can use tools to query, track, and alert on your monthly billing-related usage in the New Relic platform.

How can I be sure my data is secure?
New Relic is deeply committed to the security of your data
Our industry-standard security technologies and comprehensive policies and controls keep everything you rely on safe.
You can also make personal data requests to New Relic in accordance with data protection laws, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). In addition, you can request HIPAA- and FedRAMP-compliant data encryption and security for your eligible account and services with Data Plus and Enterprise edition.
Can I cancel my account?
You can cancel anytime with the pay-as-you-go option, and you will only pay for any usage prior to cancellation. The commitment contracts option has non-cancellable annual or multi-year agreements.
