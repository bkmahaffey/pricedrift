What if I exceed my plan limits?
You will receive a notification from our team with instructions on upgrading to a higher-tier plan. Any sessions above the limit will not be recorded.
Is discounted annual pricing available?
By default, LogRocket is billed as a monthly subscription. If you would like to pre-pay for an annual LogRocket subscription, you will receive a discount for annual billing. For annual billing options, contact sales.
