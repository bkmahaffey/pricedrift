Do you offer discounts for non-commercial projects?
Yes, we can offer a significant discount on Liveblocks for non-commercial projects. In most cases, we’ll offer a discount that allows us to cover a portion of the costs related to onboarding and customer service. Please contact us
with information and we’ll get back to you.
How are MAU calculated?
Monthly active users (MAU) is the number of users that connect to Liveblocks in a given month.
Users are identified by the userId property provided in your authentication endpoint
This userId generally comes from your database or your authentification provider. One unique userId corresponds to one user, whether they connected to a room 1 day for 5 minutes or kept coming back every day for 8 hours.
One user will be counted as one monthly active user, no matter how frequently they connect to Liveblocks rooms in any given month as long as it falls under our Fair Use Policy
.
What’s a room?
A room is the virtual space where people collaborate. For most products, a room corresponds to a document.
What’s a simultaneous connection per room?
A simultaneous connection per room is when a user or multiple users are connected to the same Liveblocks room at the same time.
What’s a simultaneous connection per project?
A simultaneous connection per project is when a user or multiple users are connected to the same project at the same time.
Do you have a yearly pricing option?
Yes, we can offer a discount if you pay yearly.
What happens when limits are reached?
Liveblocks returns a different error for each limit that a user might reach. That way, you can decide how to best handle those cases in your product. Learn more about limits.
How can I monitor my usage and billing?
You can track your usage in the Liveblocks dashboard.
How secure and reliable is Liveblocks?
Security and reliablity are one of our top concerns when building Liveblocks. We’ve designed our back-end infrastructure to be deployed on the edge using Cloudflare Workers — giving us exceptional performance, reliability, and scale. And we’ve baked authentication into the platform, so that you can set custom permissions for your users. Liveblocks is also SOC 2 compliant. Make sure to check out our Security page
.
