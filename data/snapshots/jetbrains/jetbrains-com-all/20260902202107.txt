FAQ
Does the JetBrains All Products Pack include AI tools?
Yes, the All Products Pack comes with the JetBrains AI Pro plan, which includes 10 AI Credits for individual use and 20 for organizations every 30 days, plus anytime top-ups. One credit equals approximately 10 requests to generate code in the AI chat or 25 requests to explain code fragments.
Is the JetBrains All Products Pack worth it?
If you’re using more than two JetBrains IDEs, it’s more cost-effective to invest in the All Products Pack.
What is included in the JetBrains All Products Pack?
A better question is: What isn’t? The JetBrains All Products Pack includes 10 IDEs (IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, PhpStorm, GoLand, Rider, CLion, RustRover, WebStorm, RubyMine, and DataGrip), Air, three extensions (ReSharper for Visual Studio, ReSharper C++, and dotCover), two profilers (dotTrace and dotMemory), as well as a JetBrains AI Pro subscription.
Can I install and use multiple IDEs at the same time or on different computers?
Yes, with the All Products Pack, you can use multiple JetBrains IDEs simultaneously! IDEs included in a personal or commercial All Products Pack license may be installed and run at the same time on a single machine or on multiple devices.
Under our current subscription model, you can run multiple personal licensed copies of the same IDE or different IDEs from an All Products Pack subscription concurrently, for example, on a laptop and a desktop for remote debugging client/server apps.
Can I upgrade my existing JetBrains IDE license to the All Products Pack?
Yes, if you already have a JetBrains IDE subscription, you can purchase the All Products Pack with an additional discount.
You can upgrade directly from your JetBrains Account
using the Upgrade link next to any eligible license. If you have any questions, please contact our Sales team
.
What happens when my All Products Pack subscription expires?
If you purchased a yearly All Products Pack subscription or renewed a monthly subscription for 12 consecutive months, you’re eligible for a perpetual fallback license. This allows you to continue indefinitely using the particular version available at the time your subscription started. You won’t receive updates or access to newer versions unless you renew your subscription. Read more about perpetual fallback licenses
.
Are there discounts for students, teachers, startups, or open-source contributors?
Students can apply for the free Student Pack
which includes access to JetBrains IDEs, JetBrains AI Pro, and more. Additionally, former Student Pack holders enjoy 40% off the All Products Pack after they graduate. For educators, there’s the JetBrains Teacher Pack
and core maintainers of open-source projects are eligible for complimentary All Products Pack subscriptions. Read more on our support for open-source projects
.
What’s the difference between the All Products Pack and IntelliJ IDEA?
IntelliJ IDEA is a single IDE primarily focused on Java and JVM-based development, with additional support for languages such as Python, JavaScript, PHP, and SQL.
The All Products Pack is a subscription that provides access to multiple JetBrains IDEs, tools, and AI features, including IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, PhpStorm, CLion, Rider, GoLand, DataGrip, and more. The All Products Pack allows you to choose the best JetBrains product for each language or project, rather than being limited to a single tool.
