FAQs
How does per-second pricing work? You are charged based on the time it takes your code to execute, prorated to the second. The price depends on the machine types you use in each stage of your CI/CD pipelines. Consistent month-to-month usage results in a consistent invoice.
What is the difference between Startup and Scaleup? The Scaleup plan comes with a batch of advanced security and compliance features that are not included in the Startup plan. It provides an additional level of tech. and account support as well as additional discounts and benefits.
What is the difference between Cloud and Hybrid? Cloud plans have no per-seat cost but they don’t include access to self-hosted agents. Hybrid plans on the other hand provide you access to self-hosted agents but also include seat cost. Other than that, both plans have access to identical in-app features.
What is the difference between Hybrid (self-hosted) and On-Premise? On the Hybrid plan, you will be using our Cloud app in combination with your agents, so the jobs run in your environment, but the app is still hosted on our end. With the On-Premise option, everything is fully hosted on your end, Semaphore app included.
Do you offer annual plans? Yes, we do, please reach out to your Customer Success team representative or contact us
for further specifics. All annual plans come with a discount.
Can I use Semaphore 2.0 for my open-source project? Linux and macOS open-source projects get free parallel CI on Semaphore.

How does Semaphore keep my code safe? We take the security of your company’s source code very seriously. Our Security page
outlines how we ensure your code is safe.
What forms of payment do you accept? We accept all major credit cards. For additional payment options, like wire transfers, please contact our customer success team.
