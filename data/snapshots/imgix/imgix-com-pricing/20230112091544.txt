Pricing Frequently
Asked Questions
What is an Origin Image?
An Origin Image is a unique asset requested by imgix from your image storage (e.g. an S3 bucket). Transformations of Origin Images are called derivatives.
Learn More
What do you charge for bandwidth?
Bandwidth is included in all imgix plans, including our Free plan.
Excessive bandwidth usage above 100 MB per month per Origin Image included in each plan will be monitored by our team. Customers who need more than this can upgrade or reach out to our sales team about a custom Enterprise Plan.
How and when will I be billed?
We bill on the first of each month for that month's usage. Origin Image counts also reset then.
If you have a credit card on file with us, that card will automatically be charged and a receipt will be emailed to you.
For enterprise customers on invoice pricing, an invoice will be emailed to the billing contact on the account.
What happens if I go over my included Origin Images?
Free plans:
New Origin Images past the limit will not be rendered. Previously cached derivatives and derivatives of previously cached Origin Images will continue to be served but we cannot guarantee how long Origin Images or their derivatives will stay in cache. Consider upgrading your plan to guarantee new Origin Images are served.
All other plans:
Origin Image limits are soft limits. You'll be encouraged to upgrade your plan if you exceed the limit, and if you exceed the limit consistently, we'll ask you to upgrade in order to continue your service. If your usage requires an Enterprise Plan, our sales team will reach out to discuss your needs.
I'm an existing customer. How do I switch to one of these pricing plans?
Please reach out to us at support@imgix.com
and we would be happy to assist you.
Can I upgrade/downgrade anytime?
Upgrade or downgrade your plan whenever you choose. Upgrades take effect immediately and will be prorated to the current month.
Downgrades will be effective at the next billing cycle (beginning of the next month for monthly plans, beginning of the next year for annual plans).
How do I cancel?
You can cancel your self-service plan at any time.
Cancellations take effect at the next billing cycle (beginning of the next month for monthly plans, beginning of the next year for annual plans).
