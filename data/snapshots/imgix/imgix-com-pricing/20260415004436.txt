FAQ
What are credits?
Credits are a measure of capacity that are consumed by media management, delivery, and transformation.
Why is Imgix moving to a credits-based pricing model?
We moved to a credits-based system so every customer can access the full Imgix platform. Our previous pricing model, which was tied to origin images, made it difficult to offer newer capabilities like video and AI transformations to all customers in a consistent, scalable way. The new model gives you access to everything Imgix offers and provides a clearer, more flexible way to understand how your usage translates into spend.
How does the credit system work?
At the start of each billing period, your bundle includes a set number of credits to use across media management, delivery, and transformations. If you're on a monthly self-serve bundle, your credits refresh each month. If you're on an annual contract bundle, they refresh once per year.
Will any API calls or SDKs change with the new pricing model?
No. There are no changes to our API endpoints or SDKs as part of this pricing update. Your existing integrations will continue to work as they are.
