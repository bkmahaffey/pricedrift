Just-in-time encoding
When preparing a video for streaming, the standard practice is to first encode the whole video into a compatible format and file size, as well as smaller size versions for slower networks. Mux instead has built just-in-time encoding, meaning we only create streaming versions of your video when a viewer is going to watch them. This saves on encoding and storage costs, which we pass on to you with lower input and storage prices. This is also how we offer great features like super fast upload-to-pl
and instant clipping
.

What is video quality?
Quality refers to how faithfully the delivered video matches your source at the same resolution. Higher quality uses more bits and increases costs, but can be beneficial for high-production-value videos. Learn more
.
Basic
Mux intelligently analyzes video content and automatically applies the right bitrate for efficient streaming. Supports on-demand video only, up to 4K.
Great for social or user-generated content.
Plus
Smart bitrate selection with increased bitrates for higher visual quality. Supports live video up to 1080p, on-demand video up to 4K.
Great for professional or branded content.

What is Mux Robots?
Mux Robots hosts AI workflows for your Mux assets. With simple API calls (or directly in the Mux dashboard) you can summarize a video, moderate it, translate captions, generate chapters, find key moments, and much more. If you already have a preferred AI vendor or want to build your own, use @mux/ai. Learn more
.
