Billing example 1
Assuming that your usage in a given month is 90 GB and you are on the Pro plan. Bandwidth included in Pro plan = 225 GB
Since your current bandwidth consumption is less than the fixed inclusions in the Pro plan, your bill would be equal to the Base price of the Pro plan = $ 89
Billing example 2
Assume you consumed 450 GB bandwidth, 250 GB media library storage, 25000 video processing units and 5000 extension units
in a given month.
Then the bill will be calculated as follows:
Fixed cost
Base price of the Pro plan = $ 89
Bandwidth
Bandwidth included in Pro plan = 225 GB
Additional bandwidth = 450 GB - 225 GB = 225 GB
Additional bandwidth units = 225 GB / 1 GB = 225 units (rounded up)
Additional bandwidth cost = 225 units x $ 0.45 = $ 101.25
Media library storage
Media library storage included in Pro plan = 225 GB
Additional storage = 250 GB - 225 GB = 25 GB
Additional storage units = 25 GB / 1 GB = 25 units (rounded up)
Additional storage cost = 25 units x $ 0.09 = $ 2.25
Video processing
Video processing units included in Pro plan = 5000
Additional usage = 25000 - 5000 = 20000
Additional units = 20000 / 10000 = 2 units (rounded up)
Additional cost = 2 units x $ 9 = $ 18
Extensions
Extension units included in Pro plan = 4000
Additional usage = 5000 - 4000 = 1000
Additional units = 1000 / 5000 = 1 units (rounded up)
Additional cost = 1 units x $ 9 = $ 9
Total Bill (excluding applicable taxes, if any)
$ 89 + $ 101.25 + $ 2.25 + $ 18 + $ 9 = $ 219.5
Is there minimum billing even if I don't use included bandwidth?
There is no minimum billing on the Forever Free plan. There is a minimum billing of $ 89 if you are on the Pro Plan. This minimum billing includes 225 GB bandwidth, 225 GB media storage, 5000 video processing units, 4000 extension units
a custom domain name and many more advanced features.
How is the bandwidth calculated for my account?
ImageKit considers the final output object size for computing the output bandwidth. For example, if your original object is of size 3MB, and you optimize it to 150KB with ImageKit, you are billed for 150KB of bandwidth. If you access this object 100 times, you are billed for 100 x 150KB ~ 15MB of bandwidth. Please refer to the FAQs related to billing calculation for more details.
How is the storage consumption calculated for my account?
Any object that is explicitly uploaded to the ImageKit media library or any video transformation generated using ImageKit adds to your storage consumption. Image transformations are not billed towards your storage consumption. Please refer to the FAQs related to billing calculation and plan inclusions for more details.
What is a video processing unit, and how is it calculated?
Video processing units used depends on output video duration and resolution:
1 second of SD video output = 1 unit
1 second of HD video output = 2 units
1 second of 4K video output = 4 units
1 second of 8K video output = 8 units
1 second of 16K video output = 16 units
Every new video transformation that has never been done before will contribute towards video processing units using the above definition. Subsequent views of the same video transformation only count towards bandwidth. Refer to the documentation
for the definition of resolution.
What is an extension unit, and how is it calculated?
Use of any third-party extension with ImageKit like Google Cloud Vision, AWS Rekogniton, or Remove BG requires extension units. Depending on the complexity of the task, each use of a particular extension might consume more than 1 extension unit. Learn more about extension units pricing
.

Do I get billed when I add my card details?
You are always billed at the first of the following month regardless of when you upgrade.
How do I get a refund?
If you are incorrectly charged in a particular invoice, you can reach out to our support team, and we will help you resolve the issue as soon as possible.
I have credits available. How will I be billed?
If you have credits in your account, they automatically get used against your invoices.
