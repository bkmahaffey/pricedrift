Free
The Free plan is for small teams of fewer than 10 people who are just getting started with Jira.
On the Free plan you get:
Up to 10 users (or 3 agents for Jira Service Management)
2 GB of file storage
Atlassian Community

Limitations in Free
There are some limitations to the following features when you use Free plans for Jira Cloud products:
Permissions, roles, and work-level security
Audit logs
Email notifications
Apps that extend Jira Cloud
Advanced roadmaps that can help you track multiple projects across different teams
If all of your Jira Cloud products are on the Free plan, those products will be deactivated after 120 days of inactivity.
Read more about deactivating inactive products and other limitations in Free plans
.
Standard
As your team grows, and you need tighter access control, more file storage, and better support, you can move to Standard or Premium.
On the Standard plan, you move up to:
Up to 50,000 users for Jira (10,000 for Jira Work Management and 20,000 agents in Jira Service Management)
250 GB of file storage
Advanced permissions, roles, and permission sharing between company-managed projects
Audit logs for keeping track of important changes
Pin product content at rest to a data realm – Learn more
Standard Support (available 9 am–5 pm Monday to Friday for your time zone)
Advanced roadmaps can help you and your team track multiple projects across different teams. To take advantage of Jira's powerful project tracking features, upgrade your plan to Premium.

The Premium plan is available for Jira and Jira Service Management. This plan gets you the best of everything, including Premium dedicated customer support.
On the Premium plan for both Jira and Jira Service Management, you get:
Unlimited file storage

99.9% uptime service level agreement (SLA)
Advanced roadmaps that help you track multiple projects across different teams

Enterprise
The Enterprise plan is available for Jira and Jira Service Management. This plan gets you everything from the Premium plan, plus you can:
Give users access to multiple Jira Enterprise products (up to 150) and only pay for each user once
Get our 99.95% uptime service level agreement (SLA)
To inquire about an Enterprise trial or change to an Enterprise plan, fill out our contact form
and someone from our team will reach out.
Inherited Jira Work Management plans
If you have a license for either Jira or Jira Service Management, you also get Jira Work Management with project templates made specifically for business teams. The Jira Work Management plan you inherit depends on what plans you have for your other Jira Cloud products.
If all of your other Jira Cloud products are on the Free plan, you'll get the features of a Jira Work Management Free plan
If all of your other Jira Cloud products are on the Standard plan, you'll get the features of Jira Work Management Standard plan
If all of your other Jira Cloud products are on the Premium plan, you'll get the features of a Jira Work Management Premium plan
Currently, Jira Work Management does not have Enterprise plans.
If you don't specifically activate a license for Jira Work Management, you can use the features associated with your plan level but you don't get a separate user allowance for Jira Work Management. To add more users for Jira Work Management, activate a plan for Jira Work Management.
Change your plan
If you're using Jira Cloud already, you can try another plan at no extra cost for the remainder of your current and next billing cycles, and cancel any time during the trial if you don't want to proceed. During a trial, you can use all the features available on the new plan, but SLA compensation included on the Premium or Enterprise plan doesn't commence until the end of your trial. Once the trial finishes, you'll be placed onto the new plan and charged the new amount at the end of your billing 
to try the Enterprise plan.
Not sure which plan you're on? Choose Settings () > Billing > Manage subscriptions to find out. The plan for each product is shown next to the product name.
If you change to a lower plan level, you can keep using the features available in your current plan until the end of your billing period.
You need to be an Atlassian Cloud site admin to change a plan for your site. Learn more about site admins
.
To change the plan for your site:
Choose Settings () > Billing > Manage subscriptions
Choose Change next to your plan name

To successfully change to the Free plan for a Jira Cloud product, you need to have 10 users or fewer ( Jira) or 3 agents or fewer (Jira Service Management). If you're over that limit when you try to change your plan, the change will fail and you'll remain on your current plan. Jira Work Management
Free cloud migration trials for Jira Data Center instances
If you're using Jira Data Center and trialing Jira Cloud, your cloud migration trial lasts for the remainder of your existing data center license period, up to 12 months. If you change to the Free plan during that trial period, your cloud migration trial will end immediately, and you'll be required to upgrade to a Standard or Premium Jira Cloud plan to use additional features.
