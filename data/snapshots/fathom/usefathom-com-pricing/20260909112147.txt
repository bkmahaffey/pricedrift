Frequently asked questions
Do you offer sales calls or live demos?
We don’t offer sales calls or live demos. You can watch our 10-minute walkthrough
explore the live dashboard
or try Fathom free for 7 days alongside your existing analytics. For help, browse the documentation
or email us
.
Do you offer a free plan?
We don’t offer a free plan. Subscription fees pay for running and improving Fathom. We don’t sell your data.
Do you run sales or offer discounts?
We don’t run seasonal sales. Annual billing includes two months free, and signing up through an affiliate gives you a $10 credit toward your first invoice.
What happens if I hit my pageview limit?
Occasional traffic spikes don’t automatically stop your analytics. For substantial or sustained overages, we email you to request an upgrade. You approve plan changes yourself. Unresolved overages can restrict dashboard and API access while collection continues. See our plan-limits guide
for the details.
Can I change my pageview limit?
Yes. If you exceed your limit for two months in a row, we’ll contact you about a larger plan. If your usage stays below your current tier for two months, you can choose a smaller plan in billing settings, unless you’re already on the lowest tier.
How many sites does my plan include?
Select a plan above to see its included sites. Every plan includes at least 50. You can add up to 15 packs of 50 extra sites in billing settings, at $10/month or $100/year per pack. For more than 750 extra sites, contact us
.
How do I cancel my account or trial?
Cancel anytime in your account settings. No call or email required. You can export your data before cancelling.
Do custom events count towards my pageview limit?
Yes. Each custom event counts towards your monthly pageview allowance, including events sent through the API.
