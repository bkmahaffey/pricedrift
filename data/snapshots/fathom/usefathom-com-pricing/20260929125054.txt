Frequently asked questions
How does the 7-day trial work?
Choose a plan and add your card to start a 7-day free trial. Your card is not charged until the trial ends, and we’ll email you before your plan starts billing. Your subscription then renews automatically unless you cancel in your account settings. You can run Fathom alongside your existing analytics while you try it.
How do I cancel my account or trial?
We’re not interested in having someone’s money if they aren’t happy. You can cancel anytime, right from your account settings. You don’t have to call or email us (unless you have a question). There are no contracts, and you can export your data before cancelling.
What happens if I hit my pageview limit?
We’ll never turn your analytics off for occasional traffic spikes. If you stay well over your limit, we’ll email you about a larger plan, and you decide whether to upgrade or cancel (no hard feelings either way). We send a few friendly reminders and extra time before we pause dashboard and API access, and we keep collecting your data throughout. See our plan-limits guide
for the details.
Do custom events count towards my pageview limit?
Yes. If you use custom events on your website or send them through our API, each one counts towards your monthly pageview allowance.
How many sites does my plan include?
Every plan includes at least 50 sites, and bigger plans include more. Select a plan above to see exactly how many. If you need more, you can add up to 15 packs of 50 extra sites yourself from your billing settings, at $10/month or $100/year per pack. Need to go really big? Get in touch
and we’ll sort you out.
Can I change my pageview limit?
Of course! If you exceed your limit for two months in a row, we’ll get in touch about a larger plan. Or, if you’re under your current tier for two months in a row (and it’s not our lowest tier already), you can move to a smaller plan from your billing settings.
Can I explore Fathom before signing up?
We don’t do sales calls or live demos, but our software is ridiculously easy to use. You can watch our 10-minute walkthrough
click around the live dashboard
or give Fathom a 7-day free trial alongside your existing analytics. Every feature is documented
and if you have specific questions, we’re here by email
.
Do you offer a free plan?
Nope, and here’s why: we’ve invested heavily in fast, reliable infrastructure (we don’t host Fathom on cheap servers that go offline). Our business model is selling software, not your data, so we’ve priced Fathom to be sustainable for a very long time.
