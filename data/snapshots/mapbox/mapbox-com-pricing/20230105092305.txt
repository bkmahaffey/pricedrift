The Nav SDK supports two types of trips: active guidance trips and free-drive trips. An active guidance trip is a navigation session that has turn-by-turn instructions to a specific destination. A free-drive trip is a navigation session that does not have turn by turn instructions to a specific destination, but still map-matches the user to a specific location and shows relevant information along their route.
Active guidance trips will appear on your invoice as Active Guidance Trips for Mobile and free-drive trips will appear as Free Drive Trips for Mobile.
