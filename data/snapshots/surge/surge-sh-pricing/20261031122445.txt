Questions Before you ask.
Is Free really free, with a custom domain?
Yes. Custom domains and their certificates are free for every account, with no trial period and no project cap.
What happens if I hit a limit?
Publishing to that domain pauses until the window passes. Everything already published keeps serving.
Is anything metered?
No. There's no bandwidth billing and no overage charge. The bill is the plan price.
How do I upgrade or cancel?
surge plan in the terminal, or surge plan --web for checkout in the browser. Cancel any time from surge plan --manage.
Do I need Pro for my agent or CI?
No. Tokens, previews and rollbacks are on every plan.
Higher limits Publishing at scale, or building a platform?
Higher publishing limits, and Surge as the deployment step inside your own product, are arranged directly. Tell us what you publish and how often.
